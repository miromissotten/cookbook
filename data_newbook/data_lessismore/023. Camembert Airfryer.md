### Ingredients
- 1 Camembert (in wooden box if possible)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- ½ tsp paprika powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme or basil (or mix)
- Optional (sweetness): honey
- Dipping vehicle: [[_2.6.1. Simple bread|bread]], baguette, [[_2.6.2. Sourdough bread|sourdough bread]], [[_5.1.9. Potatoes|bakes potatoes]], ... 
### Hardware
- Airfryer/oven
### Instructions
1. place the Camembert in a heat-resistant thingy
2. make slits in the top surface of the cheese
3. add paprika powder, garlic powder, and thyme/basil on top
4. Optional: add honey
5. Rub seasoning into the slits
6. Place in the airfryer and cook for **20–25 minutes at 180 °C** (until the top is golden brown)
7. Serve immediately with dipping vehicle
