### Ingredients
*   200g of your favorite pasta (spaghetti, penne, or fusilli work well)
*   1 cup of your preferred hummus
*   1 tablespoon olive oil
*   1 small onion, chopped
*   2 cloves garlic, minced
*   1 cup fresh spinach
*   1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
*   1/2 lemon, juiced
*   For the Balsamic Glaze (Optional but Recommended):
    *   1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
*   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
*   Reserved pasta water (about 1/2 cup)
*   *Optional garnishes:* fresh basil or parsley, red pepper flakes, crumbled feta cheese

### Instructions

1. Cook the pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of the starchy pasta water. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
2. Make the balsamic glaze While the pasta cooks, pour the balsamic vinegar into a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce heat to low and let it gently bubble for 5-7 minutes, or until it has thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon. Be careful not to let it burn. Remove from heat and set aside.
3. Sauté the vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Wilt the spinach and warm the tomatoes: Add the fresh spinach and cherry tomatoes to the skillet. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until the spinach has wilted and the tomatoes have softened slightly.
5. Create the hummus sauce: Reduce the heat to low and add the hummus and the juice of half a lemon to the skillet. Gradually stir in about 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water to thin the hummus into a creamy sauce. Continue to add more pasta water, a tablespoon at a time, until you reach your desired consistency. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Combine and serve: Add the cooked pasta to the skillet with the hummus sauce. Toss everything together until the pasta is well-coated. If the sauce is too thick, add a little more of the reserved pasta water.
7. Garnish and enjoy: Serve the creamy hummus pasta immediately. Drizzle the balsamic glaze over each serving and add any optional garnishes like fresh basil or parsley, a pinch of red pepper flakes, or a sprinkle of feta cheese.

### Extra info
* Quick Balsamic Method: If you're short on time, you can skip making the glaze and simply stir 1 to 2 tablespoons of balsamic vinegar directly into the hummus sauce along with the lemon juice.
* Experiment with hummus flavors: Try using different flavored hummus, such as roasted red pepper or garlic, to change up the taste of the dish.
* Add more vegetables: Feel free to incorporate other vegetables like sautéed mushrooms, sun-dried tomatoes, or roasted broccoli.
* Boost the protein: For a more substantial meal, add grilled chicken, chickpeas, or white beans to the pasta.
* Make it vegan: Ensure you are using a vegan hummus and omit any dairy-based garnishes like feta cheese.
