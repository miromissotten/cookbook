### Ingredients
- 240g milk (cold or ideally slightly warm (less than 40°C))
- 7g dry yeast
- 50g granulated sugar, plus 1 tsp
- 2.5g salt
- 1 egg
- 50g unsalted butter, melted
- 360g all-purpose flour (+120g)
- Oil

### Instructions
- In a bowl, mix together milk, 1 tsp of sugar, and yeast.
- Add sugar, salt, egg, melted butter and 2 cups of flour to the milk mixture
- Mix using dough mixer or by hand for about 8 minutes until elastic dough
- Place the dough into a greased bowl and cover it with plastic wrap. Let rise in a warm place until doubled. (Dough is ready if you make a dent with your finger and the indention remains. Takes about 30 minutes)
- Roll dough to about 1/2 inch thickness.
- Cut out donuts with bowls or pots or by hand in donut shapes. Transfer to lightly floured parchment paper and cover loosely with greased plastic wrap.
- Let donuts rise until doubled in volume, (about 30 minutes)
- Spray donuts with oil spray and cook at 180°C in air fryer until golden brown, about 4 minutes. Repeat with remaining donuts (and holes)
