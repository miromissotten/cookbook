# Document 1: Lemon Meringue Pie *(Preppy Kitchen)*

> *This zingy lemon meringue pie topped with a mound of light-as-air Italian Meringue all in a crispy butter crust makes for an easy but very special treat!*

* **Author:** John Kanell  
* **Course:** Dessert | **Cuisine:** American  
* **Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
* **Cook Time:** 35 minutes  
* **Chill Time:** 8 hours *(minimum 3 hours before slicing)*  
* **Total Active Time:** 55 minutes  
* **Yield / Servings:** 10 people (706 kcal/slice)  

---

### Ingredients

#### For the Pie Crust
* **300 g** all-purpose flour *(plus extra for rolling)*
* **36 g (4 tbsp)** granulated sugar
* **3 g (½ tsp)** sea salt
* **225 g** unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
* **60 mL (4 tbsp)** ice water
* **Egg wash:** 1 egg beaten with **15 mL (1 tbsp)** cream

#### For the Lemon Filling
* **5** egg yolks
* **2 g (¼ tsp)** kosher salt
* **50 g** cornstarch *(plus 8 g / 1 tbsp extra if a firmer filling is desired)*
* **250 g** granulated sugar
* **12 g (2 tbsp)** fresh lemon zest *(from 2–3 lemons; add more for extra zing)*
* **177 mL** fresh lemon juice *(from 2–3 lemons)*
* **296 mL** water
* **57 g** unsalted butter

#### For the Italian Meringue
* **266 g** granulated sugar *(divided: 66 g for egg whites + 200 g for sugar syrup)*
* **50 mL (50 g)** water
* **4** egg whites
* **1.5 g (¼ tsp)** salt
* **1 g (¼ tsp)** cream of tartar
* **5 mL (1 tsp)** vanilla extract

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Prepare & Blind-Bake the Pie Crust
1. In a food processor, combine the flour, sugar, and salt. Whiz together briefly. Add the cubed cold butter and pulse until pea-sized pieces form. Drizzle in the ice water while pulsing, then remove the blade and use a spatula to give a final mix.
2. Turn out onto a pastry mat or floured surface, knead a couple of times, and shape into a flat disk. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for **30 minutes**.
3. Preheat the oven to **220°C**.
4. On a well-floured surface, roll the dough with a rolling pin from the center to the edge (turning and flouring so it doesn't stick) into a circle at least **5 cm larger than the pie pan**. Roll the dough around your rolling pin and unroll it gently into the pie pan without stretching. Press into the pan.
5. Using a small sharp paring knife, trim the dough leaving a **2.5 cm overhang**. Fold the edge under itself and crimp the rim with your fingers or the tines of a fork.
6. Place a round of frilled parchment paper over the crust, then press aluminum foil firmly over the paper against the edges to keep the crust propped up during baking.
7. Bake at **220°C for 15 minutes**.
8. Remove the foil and parchment paper. Brush the base and edges with the egg wash (1 egg beaten with 15 mL cream) and dock the base with a fork.
9. Wrap foil strips around the edges to prevent burning, return to the oven at **220°C for 10 minutes**, then reduce the temperature to **190°C** and bake until the center is golden (**15–20 minutes**). Let cool completely.

#### 2. Make the Lemon Filling
1. Zest 2–3 lemons to obtain at least 2 tablespoons of zest. Squeeze the lemons to obtain **177 mL** of juice.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk the 5 egg yolks; set aside.
3. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, and salt. Pour in the water, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Whisk thoroughly until the dry ingredients dissolve completely without lumps.
4. Place over medium-high heat and whisk continuously until thickened (approximately 5 minutes).
5. **Temper the eggs:** Slowly drizzle approximately **240 mL** of the hot lemon mixture into the egg yolks while whisking vigorously. Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan and cook over medium-high heat while whisking constantly for **2 minutes**.
6. Remove from heat, add the butter, and stir until completely melted.

#### 3. Make the Italian Meringue
1. In a stand mixer bowl, whip the egg whites, salt, and cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Slowly add **66 g** of sugar while beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the remaining **200 g** of sugar and **50 mL** of water over medium-high heat. Stir until the sugar dissolves and becomes clear. Boil until a candy thermometer reads **113°C–116°C**.
3. Immediately drizzle the boiling sugar syrup into the mixer in a thin, steady stream with the motor running. Continue whipping until the meringue cools down to tepid/room temperature. Add the vanilla extract, beat to combine, and use immediately.

#### 4. Assembly & Finishing
1. Pour the hot lemon filling into the baked pie shell and smooth the top.
2. Spread the Italian meringue over the filling, leaving a small border of lemon curd exposed around the rim. Use a spatula or back of a spoon to create decorative swirls and peaks.
3. Toast the meringue using a kitchen blowtorch or place under a broiler for **1–2 minutes** until golden brown.
4. Chill in the refrigerator for at least **3 hours** (up to 8 hours) until fully set before cutting.

---

### Baker's Notes
* **Make-Ahead:** This pie can be made 1–2 days in advance and served chilled.
* **Extra Zing:** If you add more lemon juice, subtract an equivalent volume of water from the recipe.
* **Texture Control:** For a looser, creamier filling, reduce the cornstarch by 1 tablespoon.
* **Soggy Crust Prevention:** Do not skip the egg wash step; it creates a protective moisture barrier.
* **Alternative Crust:** This pie works well with a crushed cookie crust (e.g., lemon shortbread cookies).

# Document 2: Biscuit Cuillère *(YouSchool Pâtisserie)*

> *Technical recipe card for French spoon sponge biscuits / ladyfingers.*

* **Preparation Time:** 15 minutes  
* **Baking Time:** 8 to 10 minutes  
* **Oven Temperature:** 210°C  

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 mixing bowl (*cul de poule*)
* 1 dough scraper (*corne*)
* 1 piping bag (*poche à douille*)
* 1 plain round tip, size 10 (10 mm) (*douille unie n°10*)
* 1 rubber spatula (*maryse*)
* 1 powdered sugar dredger (*saupoudreuse*)

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Prepare a baking sheet lined with parchment paper: trace the required piping guides using a marker on the **reverse side** of the paper.
3. Preheat the convection oven to **210°C**.

---

### Ingredients
* **Egg whites:** 120 g *(approx. 4 egg whites)*
* **Caster sugar:** 100 g
* **Egg yolks:** 80 g *(approx. 4 egg yolks)*
* **Sifted flour (*T55 / all-purpose*):** 100 g
* **Icing / Powdered sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)* for dusting

---

### Protocol & Method

1. **Clarification:** Separate the egg whites and egg yolks cleanly.
2. **French Meringue:** Whip the egg whites by hand or with a stand mixer. Tighten the whites (*serrer*) by gradually adding the caster sugar in multiple additions until stiff, glossy, and firm.
3. **Incorporate Yolks:** Add the egg yolks into the meringue and fold briefly/coarsely using a rubber spatula (*maryse*).
4. **Incorporate Flour:** Add the sifted flour and fold gently with the spatula from the center outward, turning the bowl, until homogenous. *Work delicately to avoid deflating the batter.*
5. **Piping:** Immediately transfer the batter into the piping bag fitted with the 10 mm plain round tip.
6. **Dressage:** Pipe onto the lined baking sheet according to the required template (e.g., 18 cm or 20 cm discs, sponge bands / *cartouchières*, individual ladyfingers).
7. **Double Dusting (*Perlage*):** Pipe the sponge band (*cartouchière*) first. Dust generously with icing sugar using the shaker. Pipe the discs. Dust the sponge band a second time with icing sugar.
8. **Baking:** Bake immediately at **210°C for 8 to 10 minutes**.
9. **Cooling:** Transfer the parchment paper off the hot baking sheet directly onto a wire cooling rack. Once cooled, store in the refrigerator until assembly.

---

### Examination Criteria (*Le Jour de l'Examen*)
* **Texture & Volume:** The batter must not deflate during folding. It must maintain distinct, high relief lines after baking, especially along the *cartouchière*.
* **Doneness:** The biscuit must be properly baked—slightly golden on the surface and springing back (*rebondir au doigt*) when gently pressed with a finger.
* **Defects:** The biscuit must not crack, which is typically a sign of over-baking.

# Document 3: Charlotte aux Fruits *(YouSchool Pâtisserie)*

> *Technical recipe card for a classic French fruit charlotte entremet.*

* **Preparation Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes  
* **Baking Time:** 10 to 12 minutes  
* **Setting / Rest Time:** 2 hours in freezer/fridge  
* **Dimensions:** 1 pastry ring, 20 cm diameter × 4.5 cm height  

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 pastry ring: **20 cm diameter × 4.5 cm height**
* 1 strip of acetate film (*rhodoïd*)
* 1 small offset spatula
* 1 paring knife (*couteau d'office*)
* 1 serrated knife (*couteau-scie*)
* 1 pastry brush
* 1 large offset spatula
* 1 cardboard cake base (20–22 cm)

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Preheat the oven to **210°C** for the sponge biscuit.

---

### Ingredients

#### Spoon Biscuit (*Biscuit Cuillère*)
* **Egg whites:** 150 g *(approx. 5 whites)*
* **Caster sugar:** 125 g
* **Egg yolks:** 100 g *(approx. 5 yolks)*
* **Sifted flour:** 125 g
* **Icing sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)* for dusting

#### Soaking Syrup (*Sirop 1260 / Sirop à 30°B*)
* **Soaking syrup:** 200 g *(prepared by boiling equal parts water and sugar to 100°C)*

#### Fruit Mousse (*Mousse aux Fruits*)
* **Fruit purée / coulis:** 250 g
* **Caster sugar:** 90 g
* **Gelatin sheets:** 6 g
* **Heavy cream (30–35% fat):** 300 g
* **Italian meringue:** 50 g

#### Glaze & Fruit Garnish
* **Neutral glaze (*nappage neutre*):** 100 g
* **Selected fruits (fresh or frozen):** 400 g
* **Icing sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)*

---

### Protocol & Assembly

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|               DECORATIVE FRESH FRUITS & GLAZE               |
|  +-------------------------------------------------------+  |
|  |             Fruit Mousse (Top Layer)                  |  |
|  |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |  |
|C |  |       Soaked Biscuit Disc 2 (16 cm)             |  |C |
|A |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |A |
|R |  |              Diced Fresh Fruits                 |  |R |
|T |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |T |
|O |  |            Fruit Mousse (Base Layer)            |  |O |
|U |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |U |
|C |  |       Soaked Biscuit Disc 1 (16 cm)             |  |C |
|H |=======================================================|H |
|  |                 Cardboard Cake Base                   |  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Bake Biscuit Cuillère:**  
   * Pipe one sponge band (*cartouchière*) **60 cm long × 4.5 cm wide**, and two discs **16 cm in diameter**.  
   * Dust the cartouchière twice with icing sugar.  
   * Bake at **210°C for 10 to 12 minutes**. Cool on a rack.
2. **Prepare Soaking Syrup:** Prepare and cool **200 g** of syrup 1260.
3. **Prepare Fruit Mousse:** Prepare the fruit mousse according to the dedicated mousse technical protocol.
4. **Prepare Fruit Garnish:** While elements cool, dice larger fruits into neat, even pieces.
5. **Ring Assembly Setup:**  
   * Place the 20 cm pastry ring onto a cardboard cake base.  
   * Line the inside circumference of the ring with acetate film (*rhodoïd*).
6. **Trimming the Cartouchière:**  
   * Using a serrated knife, trim one long edge of the sponge band perfectly straight (trimming the less uniform side). This clean edge will form the bottom base.
7. **Layering the Charlotte:**  
   * Place the sponge band inside the ring with the powdered-sugar side facing outward against the acetate. Trim the ends so they meet flush without overlapping.  
   * Place the first **16 cm biscuit disc** at the bottom, fitting it tightly against the base of the cartouchière.  
   * Brush the base disc generously with the **syrup 1260** using a pastry brush.  
   * Pour a first layer of fruit mousse over the sponge base.  
   * Scatter the prepared fruit pieces evenly across the mousse.  
   * Place the second **16 cm biscuit disc** on top; press lightly across the surface to expel trapped air bubbles. Soak the top disc with syrup 1260.  
   * Pour the remaining fruit mousse to fill the ring.  
   * Smooth the surface flush with the rim using a large offset spatula.  
   * Transfer to the freezer for **2 hours** to allow the gelatin and mousse to set firmly.
8. **Final Decoration:**  
   * Warm the neutral glaze (**100 g**) to 30°C–35°C (color with food dye if required by the exam topic).  
   * Apply glaze over the top and arrange fresh fruits decoratively.  
   * Keep refrigerated until service.

---

### Pro Tip (*Astuce*)
If your baking trays are not wide enough to pipe a single 60 cm strip:
* Pipe the band in **two pieces**: make the first piece as long as the baking sheet allows, and make the second piece the exact remaining length so their combined total equals **60 cm**.  
* Join the seams tightly against the ring during assembly to keep the joint invisible.

# Document 4: La Mousse aux Fruits *(YouSchool Pâtisserie)*

> *Technical recipe card for gelatin-set fruit mousse with Italian meringue and whipped cream.*

* **Preparation Time:** 30 minutes  
* **Cooking Time:** 10 minutes  
* **Yield / Capacity:** Standard batch fills one entremet (Charlotte or Bavarois) measuring **18 cm × 4.5 cm** *(or a 20 cm Charlotte when combined with fruit inserts)*.

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 stand mixer / planetary mixer
* 1 wire whisk
* 1 rubber spatula (*maryse*)
* 2 mixing bowls (*culs de poule*)
* 1 saucepan
* 1 digital probe thermometer

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Place the stand mixer bowl and whisk attachment into the refrigerator/freezer to chill.
3. Bring butter to ambient room temperature (if required for auxiliary preparations).

---

### Ingredients
* **Fruit coulis or purée:** 250 g *(divided: ~125 g + ~125 g)*
* **Caster sugar:** 90 g
* **Leaf gelatin:** 6 g
* **Full-fat heavy whipping cream (min. 30–35% fat):** 300 g *(chilled)*
* **Italian meringue:** 50 g

---

### Protocol & Method

1. **Hydrate Gelatin:** Submerge the 6 g of gelatin sheets in a bowl of ice-cold water for 5 to 10 minutes to soften.
2. **Heat Coulis:** In a saucepan, combine approximately half of the fruit purée (**~125 g**) with the **90 g** of caster sugar. Whisk together over medium heat.
3. **Simmer:** Bring the mixture to a gentle boil; it only needs to be hot enough to fully melt the gelatin.
4. **Dissolve Gelatin:** Thoroughly squeeze excess water out of the softened gelatin sheets by hand. Whisk the gelatin directly into the hot coulis until completely dissolved.
5. **Temper Coulis:** Stir in the remaining cold half of the fruit purée (**~125 g**). 
6. **Cooling:** Allow the purée mixture to cool at room temperature until the thermometer registers between **28°C and 35°C**.
7. **Add Meringue:** Gently fold the **50 g of Italian meringue** into the cooled fruit purée using a spatula.
8. **Whip Cream:** In the pre-chilled mixer bowl, whip the 300 g of heavy cream until it reaches a soft, creamy Chantilly texture (*crémeuse, souple, et non grainée*—do not over-whip).
9. **Pre-Mix (*Détendre*):** When the fruit mixture is between **28°C and 35°C**, add approximately ¼ of the whipped cream into the fruit base and whisk vigorously to lighten and loosen the mass.
10. **Final Folding:** Add the remaining whipped cream into the bowl and fold gently with a rubber spatula (*maryse*) from bottom to top until completely uniform, taking care not to deflate the cream.
11. **Usage:** The fruit mousse is now ready to be poured directly into your prepared pastry ring/mold, or covered with plastic film in direct contact with the surface and refrigerated.

---

### Good to Know (*Bon à Savoir*)
* **Exam Standard:** At professional pastry examinations, fruit purée is provided in sterile Tetra Pak cartons ready for use.
* **Homemade Alternative:** To prepare fresh fruit purée from scratch using fresh or frozen fruits:
  $$\text{Sugar Required} = \text{Fruit Weight} \times 10\%$$
  *(Example: for 300 g of fresh/frozen fruit, add 30 g of caster sugar).* Heat gently in a pan until softened, blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth, and pass through a fine-mesh conical sieve (*chinois étamine*) to remove seeds and fibers.
