### Ingredients
- 1liter whole milk
- 4tbsp yogurt with active cultures (e.g., Pur Natur, plain/unflavored)
### Hardware
- Instant Pot / pressure cooker with yoghurt option
- Optional (texture): cheesecloth
### Instructions
#### Sterilize the Instant Pot
1. To the Instant Pot, add water and a a drop of soap
2. Boil for 2 minutes on high pressure
3. Discard soapy water
4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water (no scrubbing, just flush until no bubbles remain)
#### Heat the Milk
1. Add the milk to the Instant Pot.
2. Select “Boil” in Yogurt settings
3. When it beeps, wait an additional 2 minutes.
4. Remove the pot and let cool to **40–42 °C** (±30 minutes)
5. Remove the skin from the top with a spoon/ladle
#### Add Starter
1. Take out 5 tbsp of milk from the pot & mix it with the yogurt until smooth.
2. Pour the mixture back into the pot and stir gently.
#### Incubate
1. Place the pot back into the Instant Pot.
2. Set Yogurt 8–16 hours:
    - 8h → mild, slightly sweet yogurt
    - 16h → tangier, Greek-style yogurt
#### Finish
1. Remove excess water: Gently pour off liquid or strain through cheesecloth
2. Refrigerate for ≥2 hours before serving (improves texture).
### Extra info
- Storage: keeps up to **2 weeks in the fridge**.
- For thicker yogurt, extend straining time with cheesecloth until desired consistency is reached.
