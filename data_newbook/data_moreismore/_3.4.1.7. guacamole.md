### Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1/2 onion (white or red, finely diced)
- 1 tomato (seeded and finely diced)
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro (finely chopped)
- 1 jalapeño or serrano pepper (stemmed, seeded, and finely minced)
- 1-2 limes (juiced)
- salt (to taste)

### Hardware
- 1 cutting board & sharp chef's knife
- 1 medium mixing bowl
- 1 fork or potato masher (to achieve your preferred texture)
- 1 spoon (for mixing and serving)

### Instructions
- Prep the avocados:
	- Cut the avocados in half lengthwise, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh directly into the mixing bowl.
	- Immediately squeeze the lime juice over the avocado halves (this adds a bright tang and prevents them from turning brown).
- Mash and mix:
	- Mash the avocado with a fork or potato masher until you reach your preferred consistency. Leaving a few larger chunks adds a nice texture.
	- Add the finely diced onion, tomato, cilantro, and minced jalapeño or serrano pepper to the bowl.
	- Season with a generous pinch of salt.
- Final adjustments:
	- Gently fold all the ingredients together with a spoon until combined.
	- Taste the mixture and add more salt or lime juice if needed.
	- Serve immediately, or press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the guacamole to seal out air if storing for later.
