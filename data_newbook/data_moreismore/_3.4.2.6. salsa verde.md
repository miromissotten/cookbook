### Ingredients
- 400 g tomatillos, husks removed
- 1–2 jalapeños or serranos
- ½ white onion
- 2 cloves garlic, unpeeled
- 15–20 g fresh cilantro
- ½–1 lime, juiced
- ½ tsp salt, then adjust
- 1–2 tsp neutral oil, optional

### Instructions
1. Roast the tomatillos and chiles
    - Put the tomatillos, chiles, onion and unpeeled garlic under a very hot grill/broiler or in a dry skillet.
    - Turn occasionally.
    - You want blackened/blistered patches, especially on the tomatillos.
    - The tomatillos should become soft and slightly collapsed.
2. Blend
    - Peel the garlic.
    - Blend the roasted vegetables with cilantro, lime juice and salt.
    - Don't blend completely smooth; a little texture is nicer.
3. Taste and adjust
    - More lime → brighter
    - More salt → more flavour
    - More chile → hotter
    - If it's aggressively acidic, add a tiny pinch of sugar
4. Optional but highly recommended: heat 1 tsp oil in a pan and pour the salsa in. Simmer for 3–5 minutes. This mellows the raw flavours and makes it taste more rounded.
