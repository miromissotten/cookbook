### Ingredients
- 1 package extra firm tofu (must be pressed to get that "skeptic-winning" texture)
- 1-3 Tbsp vegetable oil (can be swapped for water/broth for oil-free cooking)
- 1 medium yellow or white onion (diced)
- 3-5 cloves garlic (minced)
- 2 Tbsp fresh ginger (peeled and minced)
- 1 cup mushrooms (sliced, or other veggies of choice)
- 1.5 - 2 cups tomato base:
	- Ideally a mix of marinara and crushed tomatoes for depth
	- 1/4 cup tomato paste (essential for richness)
- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 can chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
- 1-2 Tbsp sriracha (adjust based on heat tolerance)
- Spice Mix:
	- 1-2 tsp turmeric powder
	- 1-2 tsp smoked paprika
	- 1/2 tsp black pepper
	- 1 Tbsp Italian seasonings (optional, helps if using plain canned tomatoes)
- salt to taste
- Garnish: chopped cilantro/parsley and crushed peanuts

### Hardware
- Clean dish towel + 1 heavy object (cast iron pan or a stack of books)
- 1 food processor or high-power blender 
	- If unavailable: a sturdy bowl and a potato masher/fork
- 1 large skillet or saucepan 
	- A 2" deep cast-iron skillet is the gold standard here
- 1 air fryer (optional, for the tofu)

### Instructions
- step 1 of things in parallel
	- **The Press:** Wrap tofu in the towel, place on a plate, and weigh it down with your heavy object. Let it sit for 10-15 mins to squeeze out the water (crucial for crispiness).
	- **The Chop:** While the tofu is under pressure, chop your onion, garlic, ginger, and mushrooms.
- step 2 of things in parallel
	- **The Tofu:** Once pressed, cube the tofu. 
		- *Option A (Skillet):* Fry in 1-2 Tbsp oil over medium-high. Don't touch them until the bottom is brown. Flip until 3 sides are crispy. Set aside on paper towels.
		- *Option B (Air Fryer):* 400°F (200°C) for 10 mins, shake, then 5 more mins. 
	- **The Base:** In your large skillet, heat 1 Tbsp oil. Sauté onions, garlic, ginger, and a pinch of salt until translucent. 
	- Add mushrooms (or extra veggies) and cook for 3-5 minutes until softened.
- step 3 of things in parallel
	- **The Sauce:** Add tomato base, tomato paste, sriracha, and the spice mix to the skillet. Let it come to a simmer.
	- **The Secret Paste:** In the food processor, blend 1/2 of the chickpeas with the peanut butter. 
		- Add up to 1/2 cup water to reach a creamy consistency. 
		- If mashing by hand: get it as smooth as possible; a few chunks are fine.
- **The Integration:** Add the peanut-chickpea paste to the tomato sauce. Stir until it "melts" in and the color becomes uniform and creamy.
- **The Finish:** Add the remaining whole chickpeas. Simmer for 5-10 minutes.
	- If adding greens (spinach/kale), toss them in for the last 5 minutes.
- **Assembly:** Fold the crispy tofu bites into the stew right before serving (to keep them from getting soggy) or place them on top of each bowl.
- **Serve:** Top with cilantro, crushed peanuts, and extra sriracha. Best enjoyed over white rice or with a side of fufu.
