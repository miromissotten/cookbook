### Ingredients
- 4 medium tomatoes (Roma tomatoes are preferred; seeded and finely diced)
- 1/2 onion (white onion, finely diced)
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro (finely chopped)
- 1 jalapeño (seeded and finely minced)
- 1-2 limes (juiced)
- salt (to taste)
- tortilla chips (for serving)

### Hardware
- 1 sharp chef's knife & cutting board
- 1 medium mixing bowl
- 1 spoon (for tossing)

### Instructions
- Prep your fresh ingredients:
	- Chop the tomatoes, onion, and cilantro finely. 
	- Mince the jalapeño. (Keep the seeds in if you want a bit more heat, or discard them for a milder salsa).
- Combine the elements:
	- Place your diced tomatoes, onion, cilantro, and jalapeño into the mixing bowl.
	- Drizzle the fresh lime juice over the ingredients and sprinkle with a good pinch of salt.
	- Toss everything together gently until well distributed.
- Let it rest (The crucial waiting step):
	- Let the mixture sit at room temperature for about 15 minutes before serving. This allows the salt to draw out the tomato juices and blends the sharp onion and lime flavors together.
	- Give it one final stir, taste for seasoning, and serve with a bowl of crispy tortilla chips.
