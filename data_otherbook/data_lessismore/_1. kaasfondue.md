history
- 1930 Swiss cheese union popularizes cheese fondue to increase cheese consumption 
- Swiss cheese union sends fondue sets after WW2 to all military regiments & event organizer's in switzerland
- 1956: Konrad Egli introduces fondue bourguignonne to his resgtaurant
- 1965: Konrad Egli invent chocolate fondue as promotion for Toblerone chocolate

- 1/2 pound Emmentaler cheese or other Swiss cheese, grated
- 1 cup dry white wine

- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 1/2 tablespoons kirsch
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Freshly grated nutmeg

- losing a bread in the caquelon means you but the next round of drinks
- always stir in figure 8
