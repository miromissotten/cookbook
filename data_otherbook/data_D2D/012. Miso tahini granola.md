### Ingredients
- 1 cup (150 g) raw shelled pistachio
- 1 cup (140 g) raw macadamia nuts
- 1 cup (60 g) unsweetened coconut flakes
- 1/2 cup (85 g) raw sunflower seeds
- 1/2 cup (85 g) raw buckwheat groats
- 1/3 cup (115 g) maple syrup
- 1/3 cup (100 g) well-stirred tahini
- 2 tablespoons (30 g) white mellow miso
- 2 tablespoons (30 g) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon

### Instructions
- Preheat oven to 160°C (320°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Roughly chop pistachios and macadamia nuts.
- In a large bowl, combine nuts, coconut flakes, sunflower seeds, and buckwheat groats.
- In a separate bowl, whisk maple syrup, tahini, miso, olive oil, vanilla, and cinnamon until smooth.
- Pour wet mixture over dry ingredients and mix well to coat evenly.
- Spread mixture in an even layer on the baking sheet.
- Bake for 25–30 minutes, stirring halfway through, until golden.
- Let cool completely (it will crisp up as it cools).
- Break into clusters and store in an airtight container.
