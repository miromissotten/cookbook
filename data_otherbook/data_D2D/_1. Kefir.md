### Instructions
#### Make the sugar water
- Mix 1 liter water + 3–4 tablespoons sugar
- Stir until fully dissolved
#### Add the grains
- Add 2–4 tablespoons of kefir grains to the sugar water
#### Add minerals
Water kefir grains need minerals to stay healthy. Add one of:
- 1 dried fig or a few raisins
- OR a small slice of lemon (with peel)
#### Ferment
- Cover loosely (not airtight)
- Leave at room temp for 24–48 hours
