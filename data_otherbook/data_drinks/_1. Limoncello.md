### Ingredients
- 20 lemons  
- 1.5l alcohol (750 ml each) (vodka works, but 95% ehtanol works better)
- 6 cups sugar  
- 5 cups water  

### Instructions
1. Prepare the lemons
	- Wash the lemons.
	- Peel them with a vegetable peeler (avoid the white pith).
	- Save the lemon juice for another use.
2. Infuse the alcohol
	- Place the lemon peels in a large sealed container (e.g., half-gallon mason jar).
	- Pour the grain alcohol over the peels until they are fully covered.
	- Seal the container.
3. Let it infuse
	- Store in a **dark place for at least 3 weeks**.
	- Shake occasionally.
	- When ready, the peels will look pale and the liquid will be yellow.
4. Make simple syrup
	- Heat **5 cups water** until simmering.
	- Add **6 cups sugar** and stir until dissolved.
	- Let the syrup cool completely.
5. Strain the alcohol
	- Remove the lemon peels.
	- Filter the liquid through a **coffee filter** to remove bitter oils.
6. Combine
	- Mix the infused alcohol with the cooled syrup.
	- Pour into a large sealed container (or bottles).
7. Age
	- Let the limoncello rest **at least 3 weeks**, preferably **1–2 months** before drinking.
