
> *Technical recipe card for French spoon sponge biscuits / ladyfingers.*

* **Preparation Time:** 15 minutes  
* **Baking Time:** 8 to 10 minutes  
* **Oven Temperature:** 210°C  

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 mixing bowl (*cul de poule*)
* 1 dough scraper (*corne*)
* 1 piping bag (*poche à douille*)
* 1 plain round tip, size 10 (10 mm) (*douille unie n°10*)
* 1 rubber spatula (*maryse*)
* 1 powdered sugar dredger (*saupoudreuse*)

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Prepare a baking sheet lined with parchment paper: trace the required piping guides using a marker on the **reverse side** of the paper.
3. Preheat the convection oven to **210°C**.

---

### Ingredients
* **Egg whites:** 120 g *(approx. 4 egg whites)*
* **Caster sugar:** 100 g
* **Egg yolks:** 80 g *(approx. 4 egg yolks)*
* **Sifted flour (*T55 / all-purpose*):** 100 g
* **Icing / Powdered sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)* for dusting

---

### Protocol & Method

1. **Clarification:** Separate the egg whites and egg yolks cleanly.
2. **French Meringue:** Whip the egg whites by hand or with a stand mixer. Tighten the whites (*serrer*) by gradually adding the caster sugar in multiple additions until stiff, glossy, and firm.
3. **Incorporate Yolks:** Add the egg yolks into the meringue and fold briefly/coarsely using a rubber spatula (*maryse*).
4. **Incorporate Flour:** Add the sifted flour and fold gently with the spatula from the center outward, turning the bowl, until homogenous. *Work delicately to avoid deflating the batter.*
5. **Piping:** Immediately transfer the batter into the piping bag fitted with the 10 mm plain round tip.
6. **Dressage:** Pipe onto the lined baking sheet according to the required template (e.g., 18 cm or 20 cm discs, sponge bands / *cartouchières*, individual ladyfingers).
7. **Double Dusting (*Perlage*):** Pipe the sponge band (*cartouchière*) first. Dust generously with icing sugar using the shaker. Pipe the discs. Dust the sponge band a second time with icing sugar.
8. **Baking:** Bake immediately at **210°C for 8 to 10 minutes**.
9. **Cooling:** Transfer the parchment paper off the hot baking sheet directly onto a wire cooling rack. Once cooled, store in the refrigerator until assembly.

---

### Examination Criteria (*Le Jour de l'Examen*)
* **Texture & Volume:** The batter must not deflate during folding. It must maintain distinct, high relief lines after baking, especially along the *cartouchière*.
* **Doneness:** The biscuit must be properly baked—slightly golden on the surface and springing back (*rebondir au doigt*) when gently pressed with a finger.
* **Defects:** The biscuit must not crack, which is typically a sign of over-baking.
