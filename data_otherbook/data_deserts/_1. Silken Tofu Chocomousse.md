### Ingredients
- 300g silken tofu
- 100g dark chocolate (≥70% cocoa recommended)
- 10ml orange liqueur or Kahlúa or espresso
- 1-2tsp vanilla sugar (or 1tsp vanilla extract)
- tiny sprinkle of salt (two fingers grip)
- Optional (flavour, lightness): 1tsp citrus zest
- Optional (flavour) toppings: cocoa powder, whipped cream, grated chocolate, berries
- Optional (texture richness): whipped cream
### Hardware
- Blender or food processor
- microwave or small saucepan
### Instructions
1. melt chocolate 
	1. or in a small saucepan over low heat
	2. or in a microwave stirring frequently
2. blend silken tofu, melted chocolate, orange liqueur, and vanilla sugar/extract.
3. adjust to taste
4. Optional: fold in whipped cream
5. refrigerate for >2 hours (texture)
6. Serve with optional toppings
### Extra info
- warming the tofu before blending can make it smoother.
- Add ⅛ tsp cinnamon or chilli powder for spiciness
