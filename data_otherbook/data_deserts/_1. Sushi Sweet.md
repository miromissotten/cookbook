### Ingredients

- Rice
    
- Banana
    
- Strawberries
    
- Mango
    
- Chocolate spread
    
- Sweet crepe (for rolling)
    

### Hardware

- Bamboo rolling mat or flat surface for crepe
    
- Knife
    
- Cutting board
    

### Instructions

- Prepare rice (slightly sweetened if desired).
    
- Lay crepe flat, spread rice evenly.
    
- Add fruit and chocolate spread as fillings.
    
- Roll tightly and slice if desired.
    

### Extra info

- Sweet sushi is best eaten immediately to prevent crepe from drying.
    
- You can mix and match fruits for seasonal variations.
