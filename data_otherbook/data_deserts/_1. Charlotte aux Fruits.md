

> *Technical recipe card for a classic French fruit charlotte entremet.*

* **Preparation Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes  
* **Baking Time:** 10 to 12 minutes  
* **Setting / Rest Time:** 2 hours in freezer/fridge  
* **Dimensions:** 1 pastry ring, 20 cm diameter × 4.5 cm height  

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 pastry ring: **20 cm diameter × 4.5 cm height**
* 1 strip of acetate film (*rhodoïd*)
* 1 small offset spatula
* 1 paring knife (*couteau d'office*)
* 1 serrated knife (*couteau-scie*)
* 1 pastry brush
* 1 large offset spatula
* 1 cardboard cake base (20–22 cm)

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Preheat the oven to **210°C** for the sponge biscuit.

---

### Ingredients

#### Spoon Biscuit (*Biscuit Cuillère*)
* **Egg whites:** 150 g *(approx. 5 whites)*
* **Caster sugar:** 125 g
* **Egg yolks:** 100 g *(approx. 5 yolks)*
* **Sifted flour:** 125 g
* **Icing sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)* for dusting

#### Soaking Syrup (*Sirop 1260 / Sirop à 30°B*)
* **Soaking syrup:** 200 g *(prepared by boiling equal parts water and sugar to 100°C)*

#### Fruit Mousse (*Mousse aux Fruits*)
* **Fruit purée / coulis:** 250 g
* **Caster sugar:** 90 g
* **Gelatin sheets:** 6 g
* **Heavy cream (30–35% fat):** 300 g
* **Italian meringue:** 50 g

#### Glaze & Fruit Garnish
* **Neutral glaze (*nappage neutre*):** 100 g
* **Selected fruits (fresh or frozen):** 400 g
* **Icing sugar:** Quantity sufficient *(QS)*

---

### Protocol & Assembly

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|               DECORATIVE FRESH FRUITS & GLAZE               |
|  +-------------------------------------------------------+  |
|  |             Fruit Mousse (Top Layer)                  |  |
|  |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |  |
|C |  |       Soaked Biscuit Disc 2 (16 cm)             |  |C |
|A |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |A |
|R |  |              Diced Fresh Fruits                 |  |R |
|T |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |T |
|O |  |            Fruit Mousse (Base Layer)            |  |O |
|U |  +-------------------------------------------------+  |U |
|C |  |       Soaked Biscuit Disc 1 (16 cm)             |  |C |
|H |=======================================================|H |
|  |                 Cardboard Cake Base                   |  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Bake Biscuit Cuillère:**  
   * Pipe one sponge band (*cartouchière*) **60 cm long × 4.5 cm wide**, and two discs **16 cm in diameter**.  
   * Dust the cartouchière twice with icing sugar.  
   * Bake at **210°C for 10 to 12 minutes**. Cool on a rack.
2. **Prepare Soaking Syrup:** Prepare and cool **200 g** of syrup 1260.
3. **Prepare Fruit Mousse:** Prepare the fruit mousse according to the dedicated mousse technical protocol.
4. **Prepare Fruit Garnish:** While elements cool, dice larger fruits into neat, even pieces.
5. **Ring Assembly Setup:**  
   * Place the 20 cm pastry ring onto a cardboard cake base.  
   * Line the inside circumference of the ring with acetate film (*rhodoïd*).
6. **Trimming the Cartouchière:**  
   * Using a serrated knife, trim one long edge of the sponge band perfectly straight (trimming the less uniform side). This clean edge will form the bottom base.
7. **Layering the Charlotte:**  
   * Place the sponge band inside the ring with the powdered-sugar side facing outward against the acetate. Trim the ends so they meet flush without overlapping.  
   * Place the first **16 cm biscuit disc** at the bottom, fitting it tightly against the base of the cartouchière.  
   * Brush the base disc generously with the **syrup 1260** using a pastry brush.  
   * Pour a first layer of fruit mousse over the sponge base.  
   * Scatter the prepared fruit pieces evenly across the mousse.  
   * Place the second **16 cm biscuit disc** on top; press lightly across the surface to expel trapped air bubbles. Soak the top disc with syrup 1260.  
   * Pour the remaining fruit mousse to fill the ring.  
   * Smooth the surface flush with the rim using a large offset spatula.  
   * Transfer to the freezer for **2 hours** to allow the gelatin and mousse to set firmly.
8. **Final Decoration:**  
   * Warm the neutral glaze (**100 g**) to 30°C–35°C (color with food dye if required by the exam topic).  
   * Apply glaze over the top and arrange fresh fruits decoratively.  
   * Keep refrigerated until service.

---

### Pro Tip (*Astuce*)
If your baking trays are not wide enough to pipe a single 60 cm strip:
* Pipe the band in **two pieces**: make the first piece as long as the baking sheet allows, and make the second piece the exact remaining length so their combined total equals **60 cm**.  
* Join the seams tightly against the ring during assembly to keep the joint invisible.

# Document 4: La Mousse aux Fruits *(YouSchool Pâtisserie)*

> *Technical recipe card for gelatin-set fruit mousse with Italian meringue and whipped cream.*

* **Preparation Time:** 30 minutes  
* **Cooking Time:** 10 minutes  
* **Yield / Capacity:** Standard batch fills one entremet (Charlotte or Bavarois) measuring **18 cm × 4.5 cm** *(or a 20 cm Charlotte when combined with fruit inserts)*.

---

### Equipment Required
* 1 stand mixer / planetary mixer
* 1 wire whisk
* 1 rubber spatula (*maryse*)
* 2 mixing bowls (*culs de poule*)
* 1 saucepan
* 1 digital probe thermometer

### Preliminary Preparations
1. Clean and sanitize the workstation.
2. Place the stand mixer bowl and whisk attachment into the refrigerator/freezer to chill.
3. Bring butter to ambient room temperature (if required for auxiliary preparations).

---

### Ingredients
* **Fruit coulis or purée:** 250 g *(divided: ~125 g + ~125 g)*
* **Caster sugar:** 90 g
* **Leaf gelatin:** 6 g
* **Full-fat heavy whipping cream (min. 30–35% fat):** 300 g *(chilled)*
* **Italian meringue:** 50 g

---

### Protocol & Method

1. **Hydrate Gelatin:** Submerge the 6 g of gelatin sheets in a bowl of ice-cold water for 5 to 10 minutes to soften.
2. **Heat Coulis:** In a saucepan, combine approximately half of the fruit purée (**~125 g**) with the **90 g** of caster sugar. Whisk together over medium heat.
3. **Simmer:** Bring the mixture to a gentle boil; it only needs to be hot enough to fully melt the gelatin.
4. **Dissolve Gelatin:** Thoroughly squeeze excess water out of the softened gelatin sheets by hand. Whisk the gelatin directly into the hot coulis until completely dissolved.
5. **Temper Coulis:** Stir in the remaining cold half of the fruit purée (**~125 g**). 
6. **Cooling:** Allow the purée mixture to cool at room temperature until the thermometer registers between **28°C and 35°C**.
7. **Add Meringue:** Gently fold the **50 g of Italian meringue** into the cooled fruit purée using a spatula.
8. **Whip Cream:** In the pre-chilled mixer bowl, whip the 300 g of heavy cream until it reaches a soft, creamy Chantilly texture (*crémeuse, souple, et non grainée*—do not over-whip).
9. **Pre-Mix (*Détendre*):** When the fruit mixture is between **28°C and 35°C**, add approximately ¼ of the whipped cream into the fruit base and whisk vigorously to lighten and loosen the mass.
10. **Final Folding:** Add the remaining whipped cream into the bowl and fold gently with a rubber spatula (*maryse*) from bottom to top until completely uniform, taking care not to deflate the cream.
11. **Usage:** The fruit mousse is now ready to be poured directly into your prepared pastry ring/mold, or covered with plastic film in direct contact with the surface and refrigerated.

---

### Good to Know (*Bon à Savoir*)
* **Exam Standard:** At professional pastry examinations, fruit purée is provided in sterile Tetra Pak cartons ready for use.
* **Homemade Alternative:** To prepare fresh fruit purée from scratch using fresh or frozen fruits:
  $$\text{Sugar Required} = \text{Fruit Weight} \times 10\%$$
  *(Example: for 300 g of fresh/frozen fruit, add 30 g of caster sugar).* Heat gently in a pan until softened, blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth, and pass through a fine-mesh conical sieve (*chinois étamine*) to remove seeds and fibers.
