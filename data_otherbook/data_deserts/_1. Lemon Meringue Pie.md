

> *This zingy lemon meringue pie topped with a mound of light-as-air Italian Meringue all in a crispy butter crust makes for an easy but very special treat!*

* Author: John Kanell  
* Course: Dessert | Cuisine: American  
* Prep Time: 20 minutes  
* Cook Time: 35 minutes  
* Chill Time: 8 hours *(minimum 3 hours before slicing)*  
* Total Active Time: 55 minutes  
* Yield / Servings: 10 people (706 kcal/slice)  

---

### Ingredients

#### For the Pie Crust
* 300 g all-purpose flour *(plus extra for rolling)*
* 36 g (4 tbsp) granulated sugar
* 3 g (½ tsp) sea salt
* 225 g unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
* 60 mL (4 tbsp) ice water
* Egg wash: 1 egg beaten with 15 mL (1 tbsp) cream

#### For the Lemon Filling
* 5 egg yolks
* 2 g (¼ tsp) kosher salt
* 50 g cornstarch *(plus 8 g / 1 tbsp extra if a firmer filling is desired)*
* 250 g granulated sugar
* 12 g (2 tbsp) fresh lemon zest *(from 2–3 lemons; add more for extra zing)*
* 177 mL fresh lemon juice *(from 2–3 lemons)*
* 296 mL water
* 57 g unsalted butter

#### For the Italian Meringue
* 266 g granulated sugar *(divided: 66 g for egg whites + 200 g for sugar syrup)*
* 50ml (50 g) water
* 4 egg whites
* 1.5 g (¼ tsp) salt
* 1 g (¼ tsp) cream of tartar
* 5 mL (1 tsp) vanilla extract

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Prepare & Blind-Bake the Pie Crust
1. In a food processor, combine the flour, sugar, and salt. Whiz together briefly. Add the cubed cold butter and pulse until pea-sized pieces form. Drizzle in the ice water while pulsing, then remove the blade and use a spatula to give a final mix.
2. Turn out onto a pastry mat or floured surface, knead a couple of times, and shape into a flat disk. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
3. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
4. On a well-floured surface, roll the dough with a rolling pin from the center to the edge (turning and flouring so it doesn't stick) into a circle at least 5 cm larger than the pie pan. Roll the dough around your rolling pin and unroll it gently into the pie pan without stretching. Press into the pan.
5. Using a small sharp paring knife, trim the dough leaving a 2.5 cm overhang. Fold the edge under itself and crimp the rim with your fingers or the tines of a fork.
6. Place a round of frilled parchment paper over the crust, then press aluminum foil firmly over the paper against the edges to keep the crust propped up during baking.
7. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes.
8. Remove the foil and parchment paper. Brush the base and edges with the egg wash (1 egg beaten with 15 mL cream) and dock the base with a fork.
9. Wrap foil strips around the edges to prevent burning, return to the oven at 220°C for 10 minutes, then reduce the temperature to 190°C and bake until the center is golden (15–20 minutes). Let cool completely.

#### 2. Make the Lemon Filling
1. Zest 2–3 lemons to obtain at least 2 tablespoons of zest. Squeeze the lemons to obtain 177 mL of juice.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk the 5 egg yolks; set aside.
3. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, and salt. Pour in the water, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Whisk thoroughly until the dry ingredients dissolve completely without lumps.
4. Place over medium-high heat and whisk continuously until thickened (approximately 5 minutes).
5. Temper the eggs: Slowly drizzle approximately 240 mL of the hot lemon mixture into the egg yolks while whisking vigorously. Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan and cook over medium-high heat while whisking constantly for 2 minutes.
6. Remove from heat, add the butter, and stir until completely melted.

#### 3. Make the Italian Meringue
1. In a stand mixer bowl, whip the egg whites, salt, and cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Slowly add 66 g of sugar while beating until stiff, glossy peaks form.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine the remaining 200 g of sugar and 50 mL of water over medium-high heat. Stir until the sugar dissolves and becomes clear. Boil until a candy thermometer reads 113°C–116°C.
3. Immediately drizzle the boiling sugar syrup into the mixer in a thin, steady stream with the motor running. Continue whipping until the meringue cools down to tepid/room temperature. Add the vanilla extract, beat to combine, and use immediately.

#### 4. Assembly & Finishing
1. Pour the hot lemon filling into the baked pie shell and smooth the top.
2. Spread the Italian meringue over the filling, leaving a small border of lemon curd exposed around the rim. Use a spatula or back of a spoon to create decorative swirls and peaks.
3. Toast the meringue using a kitchen blowtorch or place under a broiler for 1–2 minutes until golden brown.
4. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours (up to 8 hours) until fully set before cutting.

---

### Baker's Notes
* Make-Ahead: This pie can be made 1–2 days in advance and served chilled.
* Extra Zing: If you add more lemon juice, subtract an equivalent volume of water from the recipe.
* Texture Control: For a looser, creamier filling, reduce the cornstarch by 1 tablespoon.
* Soggy Crust Prevention: Do not skip the egg wash step; it creates a protective moisture barrier.
* Alternative Crust: This pie works well with a crushed cookie crust (e.g., lemon shortbread cookies).
