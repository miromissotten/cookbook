### Instructions
##### 1. Tacos de Hongos (Mushroom Tacos)
- 400g mushrooms (button, oyster, shiitake, or a mix), sliced
- 100g onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp epazote (or 1/2 tsp dried oregano)
- 1-2 chilies (serrano/jalapeño, minced) or 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15g cilantro, chopped
- 15ml oil
- 1tsp ground cumin
- 1tsp ground paprika
##### 2. Tacos de Papa (Potato Tacos)
- 500g potatoes, peeled and diced 1cm (or mashed)
- 100g onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 chili, minced (or 1/2 tsp chili flakes)
- optional: 80g cheese (Oaxaca, mozzarella, or cheddar), shredded
- 15ml oil
##### 3. Tacos de Hongos con Elote (Mushroom & Corn Tacos)
- 300g mushrooms, sliced
- 200g corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- 100g onion, diced
- 100g bell pepper, diced
- 1-2 chilies, minced
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15ml oil
##### 4. Tacos de Coliflor Asada (Roasted Cauliflower Tacos)
- 500g cauliflower, cut into small florets (baked)
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 30ml oil
- 1 lime, juiced
##### 5. Tacos de Frijoles (Bean Tacos)
- 400g black beans or pinto beans, cooked (=200g dried, 25min HP, 30min natural release)
- 100g onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 chili, minced (or 1/2 tsp chili flakes)
- 15ml oil
- 60ml water or broth

### Description
- description: A set of vegetarian/vegan-adaptable taco fillings, each combining a base ingredient (mushroom, potato, cauliflower, or beans) with aromatics and chili for a quick weeknight or casual dinner-party taco spread.
