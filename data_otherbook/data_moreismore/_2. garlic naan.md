# Homemade Garlic Naan

* **Yield:** 12 flatbreads  
* **Prep Time:** 20 minutes (+ 1 hour rising)  
* **Cook Time:** 20 minutes  
* **Total Time:** 1 hour 40 minutes  

---

## Ingredients

### Dough
* **60 ml (¼ cup)** warm water *(approx. 40°C–43°C)*
* **12 g (1 tbsp)** granulated sugar
* **7 g (1 packet / 2¼ tsp)** active dry yeast
* **180 ml (¾ cup)** warm milk
* **185 g (¾ cup)** plain yogurt
* **500 g (4 cups)** all-purpose flour *(plus extra for dusting)*
* **1 tsp** kosher salt

### Garlic Butter & Topping
* **115 g (½ cup / 1 stick)** unsalted butter, melted *(divided)*
* **4 cloves** garlic, minced
* **15 g (approx. ¼ cup)** fresh cilantro (coriander), finely chopped
* *Flaky sea salt (optional, for finishing)*

---

## Instructions

### 1. Activate the Yeast
1. In a measuring jug or small bowl, combine the **warm water**, **sugar**, and **active dry yeast**.
2. Let it sit undisturbed for **5–10 minutes** until frothy and bubbly.

### 2. Mix & Knead the Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the **flour** and **salt**. Create a well in the center.
2. Whisk the **warm milk** and **plain yogurt** into the foamy yeast mixture until smooth and combined.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the flour well. Stir with a wooden spoon or spatula until a shaggy dough begins to form.
4. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and knead by hand for **3–4 minutes** until smooth and elastic.

### 3. Proof the Dough
1. Place the dough into a lightly greased bowl and cover loosely with a damp towel or plastic wrap.
2. Let it rise in a warm, draft-free spot for about **1 hour**, or until doubled in size.

### 4. Shape the Naan
1. Turn the risen dough out onto a lightly floured surface and gently deflate it.
2. Cut the dough into **12 equal pieces** and roll each piece into a smooth ball.
3. Using a rolling pin, roll each ball out into an oblong/teardrop shape about **5–6 mm (¼ inch) thick** and roughly **15 cm (6 inches) across**.

### 5. Cook in a Skillet
1. Heat a heavy skillet or cast-iron pan over **medium-high heat** until hot.
2. Lightly brush one side of the rolled dough with plain melted butter.
3. Place the dough (buttered side down) onto the hot skillet. Cook for **1–2 minutes** until large bubbles puff up across the surface and charred golden spots appear underneath.
4. Brush the top lightly with butter, flip over, and cook the other side for another **1–2 minutes** until nicely browned.
5. Transfer to a plate and cover loosely with a clean kitchen towel to keep warm and soft while cooking the remaining pieces.

### 6. Finish with Garlic Butter
1. Add the **minced garlic** to the remaining melted butter. Microwave for **15 seconds** (or gently warm in a pan) to infuse the garlic flavor.
2. Brush each warm naan generously with the garlic butter and sprinkle with **fresh chopped cilantro**.
3. Serve warm alongside curries, tikka masala, or dips.
