# Homemade Punjabi Samosas

A classic recipe for crispy, flaky Punjabi samosas filled with a spiced potato and pea stuffing.

* **Yield:** 10–12 samosas  
* **Prep Time:** 30 minutes  
* **Cook Time:** 20–25 minutes  

---

## Ingredients

### Samosa Pastry Dough *(Mawa/Khasta Crust)*
* **225 g** all-purpose flour *(maida)*
* **1 tsp** ajwain *(carom seeds)*
* **½ tsp** salt *(or to taste)*
* **4 tbsp (60 ml)** vegetable or sunflower oil *(heated)*
* **Water**, just enough to knead a firm dough *(approx. 60–80 ml)*

### Potato & Peas Stuffing
* **500 g** potatoes, boiled, peeled, and coarsely mashed
* **75 g (½ cup)** green peas *(fresh or frozen)*
* **1½ tsp** fresh ginger, grated
* **2–3** green chillies, finely chopped
* **1 tsp** cumin seeds *(jeera)*
* **½ tsp** ground turmeric
* **½ tsp** red chilli powder
* **1 tsp** amchur *(dried mango powder)*
* **½ tsp** garam masala
* **2 tsp** whole coriander seeds, dry-roasted and coarsely crushed
* **2–3 tbsp (20–30 g)** raisins *(optional)*
* **3 tbsp (25–30 g)** cashew nuts, chopped *(optional)*
* **10 g (¼ cup)** fresh coriander leaves (cilantro), finely chopped
* **Salt**, to taste
* **1 tbsp (15 ml)** oil or ghee *(for tempering)*

### For Frying / Baking
* **Oil**, sufficient for deep frying *(or 2–3 tbsp for brushing if baking)*

---

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Pastry Dough
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the **flour**, **ajwain**, **salt**, and **hot oil**.
2. Rub the oil into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs and holds together into a lump when squeezed.
3. Gradually add cold water, a little at a time, and knead into a **stiff, firm dough** *(a soft dough will make the crust soggy rather than crisp)*.
4. Cover with a damp cloth and set aside to rest for **20–30 minutes**.
5. Divide the rested dough into **5 to 6 equal smooth balls**.

---

### 2. Make the Stuffing
1. Heat **1 tbsp oil or ghee** in a pan or kadhai over medium heat. Add the **cumin seeds** and let them sizzle.
2. Add the **grated ginger** and **chopped green chillies**; sauté for 10–15 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the **mashed potatoes**, **turmeric**, **chilli powder**, **garam masala**, **amchur powder**, and **salt**. Stir well to combine and sauté for 2–3 minutes.
4. Stir in the **green peas**, **raisins**, and **cashews** (if using); cook for an additional 2–3 minutes.
5. Finish by mixing in the **crushed roasted coriander seeds** and **fresh chopped coriander leaves**. Sauté for 1 more minute, remove from the heat, and allow the stuffing to **cool completely**.

---

### 3. Shape and Assemble the Samosas
1. Take one dough ball and roll it out into an oval/oblong shape about **2–3 mm thick**.
2. Cut the oval in half across the middle to form **two semi-circles** *(each ball makes 2 samosas)*.
3. Lightly brush water along the straight edge of one semi-circle. Fold the straight edge into a cone shape, overlapping the edges slightly and pinching firmly to seal the seam.
4. Hold the cone in your palm and stuff it generously with the cooled potato filling, leaving about 1 cm of space at the top.
5. Create a small pleat/fold on the rounded back side opposite the seam *(this helps the samosa stand upright)*.
6. Lightly dampen the inner top rim with water, bring both edges together, and press firmly to seal the top completely. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling.

---

### 4. Cook the Samosas

#### Method A: Deep Frying (Traditional)
* Heat oil in a deep pan over **medium-low heat** *(the oil should be moderately warm, not smoking hot)*.
* Gently lower a few samosas at a time into the oil.
* Fry slowly on low to medium-low heat for **7–8 minutes**, turning occasionally, until the crust is crisp, evenly blistered, and golden brown. Drain on paper towels.

#### Method B: Oven Baking (Alternative)
* Preheat your oven to **180°C (350°F)** and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
* Arrange the samosas on the tray and brush them thoroughly with oil on all sides.
* Bake for **15–20 minutes** until crispy and golden brown.

---

## Serving
Serve hot with sweet tamarind chutney, spicy green mint-coriander chutney, and a cup of hot masala chai.
