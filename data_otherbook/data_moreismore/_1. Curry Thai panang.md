# Instant Pot Thai Panang Curry

* **Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
* **Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
* **Total Time:** 35 minutes  
* **Servings:** 4  

---

## Ingredients

### Curry Base & Chicken
* **450 g (1 lb)** boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **3 tbsp** Panang curry paste
* **1 tbsp (approx. 16 g)** creamy peanut butter
* **1 tbsp (15 ml)** soy sauce *(or fish sauce)*
* **1 tsp** sugar *(or granular sweetener)*
* **½ tsp** kosher salt
* **1 tbsp (approx. 15 g / 3 cloves)** garlic, minced
* **1 tbsp (approx. 15 g)** fresh ginger, minced
* **180 ml (¾ cup)** hot water *(divided: 60 ml for paste, 120 ml for the pot)*
* **200–250 g (approx. 2 cups)** onion, chopped
* **75–100 g (approx. 1 cup)** mushrooms, sliced

### For Finishing
* **240 ml (1 cup)** full-fat coconut milk
* **200–250 g (approx. 2 cups)** bell peppers, sliced
* **6–8** fresh basil leaves, finely chopped
* **2 tsp (10 ml)** lemon juice *(or lime juice)*

---

## Instructions

1. **Make the Curry Paste Mixture:**  
   In a small bowl, whisk together the Panang curry paste, soy sauce (or fish sauce), sugar, minced garlic, minced ginger, and salt. Add the peanut butter and **60 ml (¼ cup)** of the hot water. Whisk until smooth and set aside.

2. **Assemble & Pressure Cook:**  
   Add the chicken pieces, chopped onions, sliced mushrooms, and the remaining **120 ml (½ cup)** of hot water into the Instant Pot. Pour in the prepared curry mixture and stir well to coat.  
   * Secure the lid and set the valve to sealing.  
   * Cook on **High Pressure for 5 minutes**.  
   * When done, let the pressure release naturally for **10 minutes**, then quick-release any remaining steam.

3. **Add Vegetables & Coconut Milk:**  
   Remove the lid and switch the Instant Pot to **Sauté** mode.  
   * Add the sliced bell peppers and chopped basil leaves; stir and allow them to wilt for about 30 seconds.  
   * Slowly pour in the **240 ml (1 cup)** of coconut milk, stirring to incorporate.  
   * Let it heat through gently until hot—do not let it boil vigorously to avoid separating/curdling.

4. **Finish & Serve:**  
   Turn off the heat, stir in the **2 tsp (10 ml)** lemon juice, and serve warm with jasmine rice or shirataki noodles.
