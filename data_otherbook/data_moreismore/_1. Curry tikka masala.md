# Instant Pot Chicken Tikka Masala

An easy, flavorful Chicken Tikka Masala made entirely in a pressure cooker.

---

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Marinade
* **680 g** boneless, skinless chicken thighs (or breasts), cut into bite-sized pieces
* **100 g** full-fat Greek yogurt *(or full-fat coconut milk/cream for dairy-free)*
* **4 cloves** garlic, minced
* **2 tsp** fresh ginger, minced
* **½ tsp** ground turmeric
* **¼ tsp** cayenne pepper (or Indian chilli powder)
* **½ tsp** smoked paprika
* **1 tsp** kosher salt
* **1 tsp** garam masala
* **½ tsp** ground cumin
* **1 tsp** liquid smoke *(optional)*

### Sauce
* **1** medium onion, chopped
* **400–425 g** canned tomatoes (1 standard tin)
* **1** medium carrot, chopped
* **5 cloves** garlic, minced
* **2 tsp** fresh ginger, minced
* **1 tsp** ground turmeric
* **½ tsp** cayenne pepper
* **1 tsp** smoked paprika
* **1–2 tsp** kosher salt
* **2 tsp** garam masala
* **1 tsp** ground cumin

### To Finish
* **120 ml (approx. 115 g)** single cream / half-and-half *(or full-fat coconut milk)*
* **1 tsp** garam masala
* **4 g (about ¼ to ½ cup)** fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped

---

## Instructions

1. **Marinate the Chicken:**  
   In a bowl, beat the Greek yogurt well so it doesn't separate later. Stir in the minced garlic, ginger, turmeric, cayenne pepper, smoked paprika, salt, garam masala, cumin, and liquid smoke (if using) to form a thick paste. Coat the chicken pieces thoroughly and let rest for **1–2 hours** (or at least 15–20 minutes if pressed for time).

2. **Layer in Instant Pot:**  
   Add all the **sauce ingredients** (onion, canned tomatoes, carrot, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cayenne pepper, smoked paprika, salt, garam masala, and cumin) directly into the inner pot. Layer the marinated chicken (along with any excess yogurt marinade) right on top of the sauce ingredients. *Do not stir.*

3. **Pressure Cook:**  
   Secure the lid and set the valve to sealing. Cook on **High Pressure for 10 minutes**. When done, perform a **quick pressure release**.

4. **Brown the Chicken (Optional):**  
   Carefully remove the chicken pieces and either set aside, broil in the oven, or cook in an air fryer for 3–5 minutes to achieve charred edges.

5. **Purée the Sauce:**  
   Using an immersion blender, blend the vegetables and sauce in the pot until completely smooth and velvety.

6. **Add Dairy/Milk:**  
   Pour in the **120 ml** of cream (or coconut milk) and stir well.

7. **Divide Sauce (Optional):**  
   *Note: This recipe makes a generous portion of sauce. You can remove about half of the sauce and freeze it for later use.*

8. **Finish and Serve:**  
   Return the chicken pieces back into the sauce. Stir in the final **1 tsp of garam masala**. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro and serve hot with basmati rice or naan.
