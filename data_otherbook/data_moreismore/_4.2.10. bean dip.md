### Ingredients
- 2 cups refried beans (or whole pinto/black beans, cooked and mashed)
- 1/2 cup fresh salsa (like pico de gallo or your favorite smooth salsa)
- 1/2 cup guacamole (homemade or store-bought)
- 1/2 cup cheese (optional; shredded cheddar, monterey jack, or cotija)

### Hardware
- 1 small saucepan (only if you want to serve the bean base warm)
- 1 shallow serving dish or wide bowl (great for displaying layers)
- 1 spatula or spoon

### Instructions
- Prepare the bean base:
	- If you prefer a warm dip, heat the refried beans in a small saucepan over medium-low heat until warmed through. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 
	- Spread the warm or room-temperature beans evenly across the bottom of your serving dish.
- Layer the toppings:
	- Gently spread the guacamole over the bean layer using a spatula to avoid mixing them too much.
	- Spoon the fresh salsa evenly on top of the guacamole layer.
- Add the finishing touch:
	- If using cheese, sprinkle a generous layer of shredded or crumbled cheese over the top. If your beans are warm, the cheese will soften and melt beautifully.
	- Serve immediately with plenty of sturdy tortilla chips.
