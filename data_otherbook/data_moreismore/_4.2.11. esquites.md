### Ingredients
- 4 cups corn kernels (fresh off the cob is best, but frozen or drained canned corn works too)
- 2 tbsp butter or mayonnaise (or a mix of both to get the best of both worlds)
- 1/2 cup cotija cheese (crumbled, can be substituted with feta cheese if needed)
- 1-2 limes (cut into wedges)
- 1-2 tsp chilli powder (Tajín or ground piquín pepper work great here)
- salt (to taste)

### Hardware
- 1 large skillet (preferably cast iron to get a good char on the corn)
- 1 spatula or wooden spoon
- cups or small bowls (for serving)

### Instructions
- Char the corn:
	- Heat your skillet over medium-high heat. 
	- Add the corn kernels to the dry skillet (or with a tiny bit of butter/oil) and let them cook, stirring occasionally.
	- Cook for about 8 to 10 minutes until the corn kernels start to get nice, dark toasted spots.
- Mix and melt:
	- Remove the skillet from the heat source.
	- Stir in the butter or mayonnaise directly into the hot corn until it is fully melted and coats the kernels evenly.
	- Season with a pinch of salt to taste.
- Assemble and serve:
	- Spoon the warm corn mixture into your serving cups or bowls.
	- Top each cup with a generous amount of crumbled cotija cheese, a dusting of chilli powder, and serve with a fresh lime wedge on the side.
