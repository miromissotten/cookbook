### Ingredients
- 250g dried chickpeas (soaked overnight, drained)
- 80g (½ onion) medium onion, finely chopped
- 3g (1 clove) garlic
- 15g (1tbsp packed) fresh parsley
- 10g (2tbsp packed) fresh coriander 
- 0.25g (¼tsp, optional) cayenne pepper
- 1g (½tsp) ground cumin
- 1g (½tsp) ground coriander
- 0.25g (¼tsp) ground cardamom
- 2g (½tsp) baking powder
- 8g (1tbsp) plain flour
- 2-3g (½tsp) sesame seeds
- ½tsp salt (adjust to taste)
- 10-15ml neutral oil (sunflower or olive) for brushing/spraying

### Instructions
- create the mixture
- create 3-4cm balls
- airfry for 10-15mins
- serve with sauces
