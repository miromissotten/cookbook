### Instructions
1. Mix egg with salt & pepper.  
2. Add **5g butter per egg** to a **non-stick pan** (moderate heat).  
3. Scramble for **1–2 minutes** until jelly-cream-like texture.  
4. Spread egg evenly over the pan.  
5. Remove from heat and tilt pan.  
6. Fold omelet gently from top to bottom.  
7. Tap-tap-tap the pan to slide omelet onto plate.
