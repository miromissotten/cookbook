1.5.1. Pizza al taglio

Italy
Subgroup: main Carbs: flour Time: ??? Range: 4-16 people Premade: 00h05

Pizza al taglio is made in rectangular trays, it's thicker bread than pizza napoletana with higher hydration. It's more airy than crispy, something inbetween napolitana and focaccia. The biggest benefit is that any domestic oven can make it.

Instructions

1. Bianca: Patate e Rosmarino (Potato & Rosemary)

Ingredients

1

extra virgin olive oil

2

potatoes, sliced 1-2 mm thick

3

fresh rosemary, finely chopped

4

salt

5

olive oil

Instructions

1

slice potatoes very thinly (mandoline preferred), max 1mm thickness or the potatoes will be underbaked.

2

rinse briefly to remove excess surface starch.

3

dry thoroughly.

4

cover with a little olive oil, salt, and rosemary.

5

arrange in slightly overlapping layers.

6

first bake:

  • add potatoes
  • bake until potatoes have golden-brown edges.
7

no second bake

5. Bianca: Peperoni Arrosto (Roasted Peppers)

Ingredients

1

raw tomato sauce (thin layer)

2

red peppers

3

yellow peppers

4

olive oil

5

garlic

6

parsley

Instructions

1

Roast peppers until skins blacken.

2

Steam briefly & Peel skins & Slice into strips.

3

Marinate with olive oil and garlic.

4

first bake:

  • add tomato base
5

second bake:

  • Add peppers baking or near the end of baking.

4. Bianca/Rosso: Funghi (Mushroom Pizza)

Ingredients

1

light mozzarella base

2

raw tomato sauce (if rosso, ereplace with olive oil for bianca)

3

mushrooms

4

mushrooms

5

garlic

6

parsley

7

Olive oil

Instructions

1

first bake

  • add raw tomato sauce (thin layer) (only if rosso, ignore if bianca)
2

sauté mushrooms with garlic first to remove moisture.

3

cook until water evaporates.

4

allow mushrooms to brown slightly.

5

second bake:

  • add mushrooms

3. Rosso: Pizza Rossa

Ingredients

1

raw tomato sauce

2

crushed tomatoes

3

olive oil

4

salt

5

optional: oregano

Instructions

1

first bake:

  • add raw tomato sauce (thin layer)
  • Season aggressively enough that the tomato flavor remains pronounced after baking.
  • Drizzle olive oil
  • Bake until crust is deeply golden.
2

no second bake

2. Rosso: Melanzane e Pomodoro (Eggplant & Tomato)

Ingredients

1

raw tomato sauce layer

2

roasted eggplant slices

3

olive oil

4

basil

Instructions

1

first bake

  • add raw tomato sauce (thin layer)
2

slice eggplant 5–8 mm thick.

3

(salt lightly and rest 20–30 minutes- and pat dry) OR (10min in microwave + 5 min waiting)

4

roast or grill until lightly caramelized.

5

second bake:

  • add eggplant
