### Ingredients
- 400g pre-cooked rice
- Optional (flavour): 1  green pepper
- ½ onion
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 12 tbsp ketchup
- 5g butter
- salt & black pepper
- 4 eggs (beaten)
### Instructions
1. Dice green pepper, mince onion and garlic.  
2. Heat skillet with olive oil; cook garlic until browned.  
3. Add onion and cook until soft
4. Halfway step 4, add ketchup, simmer briefly to reduce sourness.  
5. Add green pepper and butter, mix lightly.  
6. Add rice, season with salt & black pepper, and stir well.  
7. Serve on a plate in an elongated rice pile.
8. In skillet, cook beaten eggs.
9. magic cook the beaten eggs to perfect.
10. Serve by putting egg over the rice pile.
