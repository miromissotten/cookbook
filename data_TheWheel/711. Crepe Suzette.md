### Extra info
- variatie: Lemon Chamomile and Greek Yogurt
	- To make the Chamomile Suzette Syrup.
	- Heat the butter, honey, 1/4 cup water, and chamomile in a small sauce pan over medium high heat.
	- Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat to low.
	- Simmer 5 minutes or until thickened slightly.
	- Stir in the lemon juice and cook another 5 minutes.
	- Remove from the heat and stir in the 1 tablespoon lemon zest.
	- Strain the syrup through a strainer.
