### Title
- title: Quick Japanese Broth

### Ingredients
- 1l water
- 6-8 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 10-15 g kombu

### Instructions
1. water + dried shiitake mushrooms + kombu in pot
2. heat on high fire until boiling (about 10 minutes).
3. remove kombu & turn off fire
4. wait 5 mins

### Dietary Restrictions
- Lactosefree: yes
- Vegetarian: yes
- Vegan: yes
- Glutenfree: yes

### Description
- description: You can put a lot of time into making a good Japanese stock. But at heart, it's simply making dashi, a concentrated umami source. That's this recipe. It contains all you need.
