### Ingredients
- 250 g buckwheat flour
- 500ml cold water
- 3g salt
- optional: 1 egg (helps binding)
- optional: 30g melted butter (optional but fat is delicious)
### Instructions
1. put the buckwheat flour and salt in a bowl.
2. add water and mix until homogenous
3. optional: add the egg and melted butter
4. the texture should be slightly thicker than milk. if not, add more water until it is
5. let the batter rest 30 minutes to 2 hours in the fridge.
6. fry/cook them like other pancakes
