In creation of a cooking book, I realised I often fall back to modular systems and combine how I feel like at that moment. This book contains the process through which I daily assemble food.

The [[_1. Architectural Recipes|first chapter]] contains the dishes which inspired this book. Each of these dishes can be places inside a system, and each system has a detailed description of recipes I like to make within them. The modular nature makes them endlessly adaptable to what you actually want to eat. The recipes described are the ones I like to call my fallback recipes which never fail to amaze me when I don't feel like thinking about the food of the day. These systems are the architectures I adapt for a modular flavour profile.

All the other parts of the book are chapters describing the components you can use within the modular systems. In any dish, you can replace any part with another component. This is where cooking begins to me.

The [[_2. Carbohydrates|second chapter]] contains the foundational building blocks of any recipe, the carbohydrates. It's the first word you say when describing your creation for the meal. We're talking about rice, noodles, bread, etc. Think: "I can replace bread with noodles" and "I can replace rice with tacos"

The [[_3. Binding Agents|third chapter]] contains the glue of the recipe, the binding agents. These make the dish a coherent story instead of a list of components. We're talking about broth, oils, and sauces. Think: "I can replace any sauce with another".

Using just chapters two and three, you can perfectly well create a servable dish, but adding one or more extra components is always a valuable addition. The [[_4. Component Lab|fourth chapter]] contains all components you can use to build out your dish. This chapter is more complex in taste though less complex in technique than the others as in their combinations need balancing in between themselves. The components (and their treatment) define the dish in terms of taste and also visually.

In theory, I've said everything I'd want to say in these chapters, but people who know me, know I can get caught up in a web of rabbitholes. And that I like it so much that I'll keep on digging. Since there was too much information to give up on, I wrote a [[_5. Curiosity Corner|fifth chapter]] which contains an unnecessary extra information I didn't know where to put logically.
