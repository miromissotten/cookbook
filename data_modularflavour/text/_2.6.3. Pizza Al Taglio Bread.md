### Description
- description: The bread makes the pizza. In this case, the bread is a hybrid between pizza napoletana and focaccia. It'ss a very high hydration and a nightmare to work with. Luckily it's worth the hassle and can't be bought in a supermarket.

### Ingredients
- 100% | 400g flour
	- use bread flour or tipo 00 with at least 13-14% protein
- 75% | 300g water
	- divided into 250g (initial) and 50g (to add with salt)
	- if using regular/non-tipo00 flour, use 10% less water (so 225g & 45g) as it absorbs less water
	- if it's your first time also use 10% less water, as a 75% hydration dough is tricky to work with and just not enjoyable as an experience
- 0.2% | 2g dry yeast
- 3% | 10g salt
- 3% | 10g olive oil
### Instructions
1. create the dough
	- mix 400g flour and 2g yeast.
	- add 250g of the water. knead shortly until no dry flour remains.
	- cover the bowl and let it sit for 30 minutes. (=this makes it less sticky for later).
	- mix the 10g salt with the remaining 50g water and the 10g olive oil.
	- knead the mixture into the dough until it is fully absorbed.
	- grab the edge of the dough, pull it up high, and fold it into the center. do this 4-5 times.
	- rest 30 minutes in a covered bowl.
	- fold once more
	- cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap and put it in the fridge for 12-24h
	- freeze afterwards if you want to keep it for later
2. baking
	- preheat your oven to its maximum temperature (usually 250°C)
	- take the dough out of the fridge 2 hours before baking (use about 1kg per 2000cm² surface of the tray)
	- drizzle olive oil into your oven tray
	- move the dough into the tray. do not use a rolling pin.
	- spread the dough to the corners using your fingertips gently. If it resists and shrinks back, let it rest for 15 minutes and try again.
	- baking stage 1: spread the tomato sauce (or others) on top. place the tray on the bottom of the oven for ±8 minutes. (creates crispy bottom)
	- baking stage 2: add the cheese and other solid toppings. move the tray to the middle rack for ±5 minutes until the cheese is bubbling.
