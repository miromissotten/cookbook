### Ingredients
- 500g mushrooms
- 10g neutral oil
- gochujang glaze:
	- 10g gochujang
	- 8g soy sauce
	- 30g tomato paste
	- very little garlic powder

### Instructions
- fry the mushrooms in a dry pan. It will release water which was trapped in the mushrooms, just wait until it's evaporated, then add oil
- create the sauce with all remaining ingredients
- add the sauce and bake until golden. Watch out for it not to burn to the pan. If it does start to attach to the pan, add some water to release it.
