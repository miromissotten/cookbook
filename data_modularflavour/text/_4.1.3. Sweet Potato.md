### Description
- description: Potatoes can do anything, and sweet potatoes are just cheated potatoes (though they're not really potatoes). They're easy and wonderful. More information on why they're a cheat can be found in section [[_5.1.10. Sweet Potato]].

### Ingredients
- sweet potato
	- 150g/p = side dish
	- 210g/p = part of main dish
	- 300g/p = main dish centerpiece)

### Instructions
- peel the skin of the sweet potatoes
- cut the sweet potatoes in ±3cm cubes
- put the sweet potatoes in water (covering with a few cm extra water on top) for 1+h in a 60-70°C bath (instant pot's keep warm function keeps the water around 70°C)
- drain the water (which could be used to make soups more silky)
- put the sweet potatoes in a bowl, add salt and olive oil, and shake until you see the outer later of the potatoes get more mushy.
	- This mushy part is the part which will form a crunchy crust
	- Do not overdo this to avoid the potatoes losing all texture
- put the sweet potatoes in an oven/airfryer at 200°C for about 45 minutes, turning/shaking every 15 minutes
- serve
