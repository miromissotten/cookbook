### Ingredients
- 500g of Caputo Cuoco or Caputo Pizzeria flour
- 350ml of lukewarm water
- 3g of fresh yeast OR 1g of instant/dry yeast
- 13g of salt
- olive oil
### Instructions
1. dough preparation in the bowl
    - in a large mixing bowl, pour in the water and dissolve the yeast.
    - add a few spoons of flour until you get a homogenous pancake batter-like texture.
    - add the salt and mix again. gradually add the flour until you get a semi-thick consistency. At that point, make a few folds from the outside toward the inside, then transfer the dough onto your work surface.
2. dough preparation on a surface
    - spread some flour over and in front of the dough. Knead until you get a smooth dough ball, then cover and let rest for 15 minutes in a covered bowl.
    - stretch the dough as much as possible from all sides, then fold each side over the others. Shape the dough back into a ball and cover again.
    - repeat this process 1 to 2 more times, always allowing 15 minutes of rest between each round.
    - for the last round, only make a few folds to incorporate air, then shape the dough into a ball.
    - place it in an oiled bowl sealed airtight and put it in the fridge for 18 hours (you can leave it longer, but do not exceed 24 hours).
3. shaping the final dough balls
    - after the 18-hour rise, take the dough out once it has puffed up well and shape it back into a ball.
    - make a cut and divide it into dough balls of about 270g each.
    - shape your dough balls, making sure to stretch them well from all sides, then place them in a tray previously coated with semolina flour (semola).
    - return it to the fridge for 4 hours (you can go up to a maximum of 6 hours).
    - ⚠️ **WARNING:** When you want to use your dough balls, remember to take them out 1 to 2 hours beforehand.
    - at this point you can start using them or freeze them for later
