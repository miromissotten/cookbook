### Instructions
- potatoes pre-prep
    - preheat oven to 200°C.
    - scrub the potatoes clean
    - stab the potato a few times with a fork (otherwise it might split while baking)
    - wrap each potato in their own aluminium coccoon (regular aluminium foil)
    - bake them for about 60-75 minutes
- toppings prep
    - not much to say, this step depends on the chosen topping really...
- potatoes prep
    - take the potatoes out of the oven and let them cool for a bit to not burn yourself
    - slice the potatoes open lengthwise like a sandwich, BUT don't cut them all the way through (like a sandwich)
    - fluff the inside of the potato with a fork, keeping the skin untouched
    - continue fluffing while adding 15g of butter and 30g cheese, optionally with black pepper and salt to your liking. fluff until smooth and creamy
    - all of that without burning yourself somehow. I've never managed.
- assembly
    - assemble by adding the toppings you want
    - finish on top with a sauce to your liking. Sour cream is traditional, but any combination works as well of chapter [[_3.4. Distinct Sauces|distinct sauces]].
