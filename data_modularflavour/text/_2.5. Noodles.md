Noodles are the best. They can add taste, they can add texture, they can add life. You can eat them slurping, rolling, or by individual strands. Most important of all, they can highlight your elegance or highlight your clumsiness based on the number of caused stains.

From a technical aspect, noodles are strands of dough, which remain solid when cooked in water. I have no idea how else to describe them. There's many types, but below are the 5 main types I actually use on a weekly basis:

- Ramen  
- Udon
- Soba
- Pasta (fresh & dried)
- Rice noodles

I won't go into tiny details for any of them as they all deserve a separate book (ramen lord went into great detail for ramen noodles, AlexFrenchGuyCooking on pasta, ...), + I usually just buy them because the difference in taste is negligible to me.
With the exception of fresh pasta, which is worth making yourself and will be the only recipe included here.
