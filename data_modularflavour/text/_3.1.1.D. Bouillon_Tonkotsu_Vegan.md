### Instructions
Follow all instructions of [[_3.1.1.0. Bouillon_Japanese_clear_vegan|Japanese bouillon]], but at the end do the following:
- squeeze the roasted garlic flesh out of the skins from the reserved bulbs.
- combine 250ml of bouillon, the reserved cooked garlic cloves, and 250g (±0.5 unit) [[_4.1.5. Sweet Potato|sweet potato]] in a mixer. Using the tricks described in [[_4.1.5. Sweet Potato|sweet potato]], you can improve the sweetness and overall taste of the sweet potato, though it takes a lot longer to make and for this dish would make a minimal difference. 
- blend until homogenous.
- stir this creamy mixture back into the main pot of bouillon until homogenous.

### Description
- description: Tonkotsu is a magnificent, most stinky soup that japanese ramen has to offer. It's base is gelatinous pork. Since I do not like to make things with pork, the internet was able to provide a vegan version. And it's pretty damn great. Nothing like tonkotsu, but damn tasty. This one is mainly based on the version of J. Kenji López-Alt, who made a near-perfect version to my taste.
