Every modular system contains components, This is no different. In this chapter, the solid components are described which can be used to create the dishes of chapter [[_1. Architectural Recipes]]. They can also replace any solid component described there. It's the easy "I can cook" trick to replace one with another.

In general, there are 2 subdivisions I make. There's [[_4.1. Vegetables|vegetables]] and [[_4.2. Non-Vegetables|everything else]]. I prefer vegetables because they're easy to predict in behaviour, while the non-vegetables are annoying and all behave differently. They're more complex and a headache if you're not with your full mind in it.
