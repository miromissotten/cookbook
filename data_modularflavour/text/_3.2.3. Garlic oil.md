### Ingredients
- 125ml neutral oil (vegetable)
- 3-4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced

### Instructions
- in a small saucepan, add the oil and scallions.
- cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally until the garlic looks light golden. (Takes around 10-15 minutes.) Do not overdo it, because brown garlic gets bitter...
- remove from the heat. reserve in the pot and allow it to cool
- strain and transfer oil to a melt-resistant contains. if not used immediately, store in the fridge for up to 6 months.

### Description
- description: Garlic oil makes it very easy to add a lot of flavour into a dish without any effort. Always keep it at hand somewhere and you'll be happy once per week at least.
