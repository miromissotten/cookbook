### Ingredients
- burger patty:
	- 150g ground meat (pork-beef works well)
	- 1 egg
	- 1.5g salt
	- 0.5g black pepper
	- 0.5g garlic powder
	- 5g butter
- toppings:
	- can be anything you like, but classically, it would be the following
		- 30g onions
		- 40g tomato
		- 1[[_2.6.5. Hamburger Buns|hamburger buns]]hup
- 250g potato
- [[_2.4.5. Hamburger Buns|hamburger buns]]
- ... or whatever you like ...
### Hardware
- pan
### Instructions
- if serving with potatoes, put the potatoes in the oven first as they take longer than any other part of the dish. oven-baked potatoes (unpealed) with salt and rosemary works very well.
- mix meat with salt, 1 egg, black pepper (coursely ground), a little bit of grated butter, and garlic powder
- shape to desire (I like a little cave in the middle with thumb for better heat spreading)
- season generously with salt, black pepper, and oil
- at room temperature, at high fire, put the meat in the pan and cover with a lid to trap the hot air
- optionally: add some butter to the top right before flipping
- flip when the browning has gone halfway through
- 30s before it's done, add cheese on top and recover with a lid
- toast the bun (on both sides!) with oil, salt, pepper, and butter on a high fire
- toppings
	- grilled onion
	- cabbage/salad
	- tomato
	- mustard
	- ketchup
	- or whatever you like really...
- pair with the potatoes
