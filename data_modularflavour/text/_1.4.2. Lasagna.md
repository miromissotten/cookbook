### Instructions
- bechamel
	- make a roux
	- add milk once homogenous
	- stir more or less 20 mins until stable & homogenous
	- add nutmeg & pepper
- tomato sauce
	- meat: beef + pork (1:1): high fire & remove excessive oil constantly → maillard reaction
	- mirepoix (onion+carrot+celery) (1,5:1:1)
	- white wine for deglazing
	- tomatoes + spices (lots more tomato than mirepoix!)
- pasta
	- very thin fresh pasta (thinnest stance: 6-7 on marcato)
- combine & layer:
	- first layer: bechamel 
	- then loop the following order:
		- +pasta
		- +bechamel
		- +tomatoes
		- +parmesan
	- Finish with a lot of parmesan
- bake at 180°C: 15-20min with aluminium foil on top + 40-45min without aluminium foil

### Ingredients
- 450g bechamel
	- 45g boter (+5g for smoothness if desired)
	- 45g bloem (T55/tipo0/tipo00)
	- 450g melk (cold)
- 900g [[_3.4.1.1. Tomato Sauce|tomato sauce]]
- 200g meat: beef + pork (1:1)
- pasta (400g fresh; 275g dried)

### Description
- description: each layer is a tricky balance between too much and too little sauce, too much and too little al dente, too sweet and too sour tomato sauce.
