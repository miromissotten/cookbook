### Instructions
The following is the original classic recipe, but i wouldn't bother as it's hard to nail perfectly each time and depends a lot on the amount of starch released by the pasta. I suggest the cheating recipe below it or a more fancy version below that one. 

#### Original aglio e olio:
- start cooking pasta in as little water as possible, lightly salted
- when 2 minutes remaining on pasta, heat up olive oil (medium heat) and add garlic (slices of garlic)
- fry until starting to golden slightly
- add pasta directly to frying pan + add a ladle of pasta water
- Once the water is close to gone but yet some remains, add cheese (choose one or add multiple)
- Stir until a sauce is formed
- grind black pepper if desired
- parsley to finish

#### Cheated aglio e olio:
- lookteentjes (in dunne schijfjes) bakken tot lichtgouden schijn in olijfolie OF look in 2 snijden en in oven laten bakken voor 10min op 180°C.
- mix 20ml water with 2g corn starch and put it in the microwave for about 20s.
- add the garlic cloves to the mixture and blend in glass until smooth (in a glass because I'm usually making this for 2-3 people and it's impossible to blend in another vessel for so few cloves and water... Ask me how I found out
- add the cheese to the mixture and blend again
- cover the pasta with the mixture
- serve with black pepper + parsley on top

#### Sweetened aglio e olio
- cook the garlic cloves for 30minutes (this release a very sweet taste)
- bake the garlic cloves with some oil and paprikapowder in an oven until slightly golden
- continue like the cheatversion

### Ingredients
- 100g spaghetti (dry)
- 8-10g (±2-3 cloves) Garlic
- 25g extra virgin olive oil
- 4g fresh parsley
- 0.5-1g red chilli flakes
- 10g salt per liter of water
- 10-15g cheese
	- (naar keuze mix, pecorino=sterk en droog, grana padano=zacht en smeuig, parmigiano=compromis neigend naar grana padano)
