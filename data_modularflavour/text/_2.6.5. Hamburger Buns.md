### Ingredients
- 350g bread flour
- 150ml room-temperature milk
- 7g dry yeast
- 20g sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 40g butter
- 1 extra egg for egg wash
- sesame seeds

### Instructions
- put warm milk, sugar, and yeast in a bowl and mix until homogenous.
- add flour, salt, and the egg
- knead for 5 minutes (use a stand mixer if you're fancy and you've got one), then add softened butter piece by piece
	- -> knead until dough is smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky
- cover with a damp towel and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour
- re-knead for 10 seconds to remove the air and then divide into 8 equal pieces
- place the pieces shaped into balls on a parchment paper sheet.
	- -> let rise for another 45 minutes (they should double in size)
- for the glossy fastfood-chain effect, whisk the extra egg with some water and brush over the buns; afterwards, sprinkle with sesame seeds
- bake at 200°C for around 15 minutes until deep golden brown

- description: If you've ever made hamburgers, you've probably made it with store-bought buns. The glossy pre-split buns which taste like sugar and nothing. I've never enjoyed a hamburger made with those buns. So, this is an easy recipe to improve your hamburgers 5-fold (easily).
