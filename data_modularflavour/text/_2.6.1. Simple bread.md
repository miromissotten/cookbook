### Ingredients
- 240 ml water
- 5 g dried yeast
- 50 g bread flour
- Optional (sweetness & fluffiness): 10 g sugar
- 350 g bread flour (for main dough)
- 2 tbsp oil (≈30 ml)
- 5 g salt
- optional extras:
    - A: seeds 60 g (sunflower, pumpkin, etc.), raisins, or mix
    - B: dried parsley 2 g        
    - C: dried beer malt 60 g (only applicable if you've made [[106. Beer|beer]] before and have kept and dried the leftover malt. It adds a maltiness, crust crunchiness and sweetness)
    - D: chopped dried apricots, raisins, butter (all to taste)
### Hardware
- mixing bowl
- oven or bread machine
- optional if oven is used (for shape): loaf pan or baking tray
### Instructions
- in mixing bowl 1, add water, yeast, 50 g flour, and sugar
- mix until homogenous mixture.
- Add 350 g flour, oil. 
- Add the salt on top of the flour (making sure to not mix it in the liquid at this stage as it might kill the yeast)
- Add extras
- If working with bread machine,
	- let it knead for 20 seconds, then add salt
	- let it do its program.
- If working manually with oven: 
	- knead for 15 minutes until smooth and elastic and it doesn't stick to your hands anymore.
	- Cover and let dough double in size (1–2 h).
	- Form the bread by hand to desired shape, let rise 20–30 min.
	- Preheat oven to 200 °C.
	- Bake 25–35 min until golden brown.
- Remove from pan and let cool before slicing.
### Extra info
- If not sure if your yeast is still alive, wait 10 minutes after step 1. If foam appears, it's alive, otherwise, it's dead and should be replaced
- Beer malt gives a slightly darker crust and malty aroma — adjust amount based on preference.
