### Instructions
There are many ways you can fill a pizza al taglio. The main idea is to overfill it. Put a lot of toppings on it to compensate for the thick dough. My favourite is potato and rosemary, so I'll start with that one. 
#### Bianca: Patate e Rosmarino (Potato & Rosemary)
##### Ingredients
- extra virgin olive oil
- potatoes, sliced 1-2 mm thick
- fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- salt
- olive oil
##### Instructions
1. slice potatoes very thinly (mandoline preferred), max 1mm thickness or the potatoes will be underbaked.
2. rinse briefly to remove excess surface starch.
3. dry thoroughly.
4. cover with a little olive oil, salt, and rosemary.
5. arrange in slightly overlapping layers.
6. first bake at 200°C with
	1. potatoes
	2. bake until potatoes have golden-brown edges.
7. no second bake
#### Bianca: Peperoni Arrosto (Roasted Peppers)
##### Ingredients
- raw tomato sauce (thin layer)
- red peppers
- yellow peppers
- olive oil
- garlic
- parsley
##### Instructions
1. roast peppers until skins blacken
2. peel skins & slice into strips
3. Optional: marinate with olive oil and garlic
4. first bake at 200°C with
	1. tomato base
5. second bake at 200°C with
	1. peppers near the end of baking.
#### Bianca/Rosso: Funghi (Mushroom Pizza)
##### Ingredients
- light mozzarella base
- raw tomato sauce (if rosso, ereplace with olive oil for bianca)
- mushrooms
- mushrooms
- garlic
- parsley
- olive oil
##### Instructions
1. first bake at 200°C with
	1. raw tomato sauce (thin layer) (only if rosso, ignore if bianca)
2. sauté mushrooms with garlic
	1. first to remove moisture.
	2. cook until water evaporates.
	3. allow mushrooms to brown slightly.
3. second bake: at 200°C with
	1. mushrooms
#### Rosso: Pizza Rossa
##### Ingredients
- raw tomato sauce
- crushed tomatoes
- olive oil
- salt
- optional: oregano
##### Instructions
1. first bake at 200°C with
	1. raw tomato sauce (thin layer)
	2. season aggressively enough that the tomato flavor remains pronounced after baking.
	3. drizzle olive oil
	4. bake until crust is deeply golden.
2. no second bake
#### Rosso: Melanzane e Pomodoro (Eggplant & Tomato)
##### Ingredients
- raw tomato sauce layer
- roasted eggplant slices
- olive oil
- basil
##### Instructions
1. first bake with
	1. raw tomato sauce (thin layer)
2. slice eggplant 6mm thick and dry them out with one of following methods
	1. salt lightly, rest 25 minutes and pat dry
	2. 10min in microwave + 5 min waiting
3. roast or grill the eggplant with olive oil until lightly caramelized.
4. second bake at 200°C with
	1. eggplant
#### Rosso: Margherita
##### Ingredients
- tomato sauce
- mozzarella
- fresh basil
- olive oil
##### Instructions
1. first bake at 200°C with
	1. tomato sauce
2. second bake at 200°C with
	1. mozzarella
3. Finish with basil and olive oil

### Description
- description: Pizza al taglio is made in rectangular trays, it's thicker bread than pizza napoletana with higher hydration. It's more airy than crispy, something inbetween napolitana and focaccia. The biggest benefit is that any domestic oven can make it.
