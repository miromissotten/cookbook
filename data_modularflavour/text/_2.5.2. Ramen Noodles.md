### Title
- title: Ramen Noodles

### Ingredients
- 90g all-purpose flour
- 10g rice flour
- 47g water (depends on the used flour)
- 1.5g kosher salt
- 1g [[_5.1.5. Kansui|kansui]]
- cornstarch

### Instructions
The recipe works for the flour I have and makes it kneadable by hand, but could need adjustment for other flours as all flours behave a bit differently.
- mix the salt and water together until the salt is completely dissolved.
- gradually distribute the salted water evenly into the flour mix, mixing with your hands until no dry flour is left. 
- shape into a ball, place in a large, durable plastic bag (leave a small air gap), and let it rest for 30 minutes.
- place the bag between two kitchen towels on the floor and step on it with your heels, flattening and stretching the dough from the center to the edges until completely flat.
- remove the dough, fold the edges to the middle, form it into a ball again, and put it back in the bag.
- repeat this stepping and folding process 4 more times until the dough is as soft as an earlobe.
- shape into a final ball, put it back in the bag, and let it rest on the counter for 2-3h.
- dust a work surface with cornstarch. Flatten the dough with your hands, then use a rolling pin to roll it into a 3mm thick rectangle. Dust with starch as needed.
- fold the dough in half or thirds, sprinkling starch between the layers. 
- cut the folded dough into 3mm wide noodles with a sharp knife.
- fluff the noodles with your fingers to ensure they are coated in starch and don't stick.
- freeze for the future or make for now
- boil in a large pot of unsalted water for about 10 minutes.
- drain and rinse well under cold running water.
