### Hardware

### Ingredients
- soup
	- 500g mushrooms
	- 1 onion
	- 4 cloves of garlic
	- 2cm ginger (or 1g dry ginger spice)
	- 4-5tbsp white [[_5.1.1. Miso|miso]] (red would be too overpowering)
	- 1tbsp tahini
	- 2tbsp neutral oil (for texture)
	- 3tbsp [[_5.1.3. Soy Sauce|soy sauce]]
	- 1l soy milk
- non-soup
	- 250g hard tofu
	- 250g asparagus
	- 100g edamame
	- 130g [[_2.5.3. Udon Noodles|udon noodles]]

### Instructions
- assemble all ingredients
- fry all soup ingredients (except the soy milk) in one pot for 10-15 minutes
	- -> blend all ingredients for the soup (except the soy milk)
- once silky smooth, bake the mixture in a pan for 5-10 minutes (continuously stirring to avoid burning the paste)
- add soy milk little by little (but don’t let it heat too much because otherwise the milk curdles and is hard to bring back to life).
- blend after a first few scoops are added. The better it's blended, the better the texture will be. 
- fry asparagus & edamame (good with some chilli flakes)
	- -> set aside
- make udon according to package
- put chilli, garlic, salt & lemon juice in a pan for 1 minute
	- -> set aside
- fry tofu with soy sauce
- -> assemble all in bowls to serve
