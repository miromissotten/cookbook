### Instructions
The amounts below are per person. Timings are for the pressure cooker unless stated otherwise.
#### Jasmin rice
Jasmin rice is the default rice for Thai dishes. Very perfumed which complements spicy food. You might sometimes read pandan rice, which refers to fragrant rice. Though pandan rice can refer to a whole group of rice types. It is named after the strongly aromatic pandan leaves,which are often used in East-Asian cooking. 
##### Ingredients
- 86-90g rice + 144-140g water per person
##### Instructions
1. 3min high pressure + 10min natural release
#### Basmati rice
Basmati rice can be compared to a less sweet, more nutty version of jasmin rice. It can be interchanged with jasmin rice and won't make a huge difference.
##### Ingredients
- 86-90g rice + 144-140g water per person
##### Instructions
1. 3min high pressure + 10min natural release
#### Sushi rice
Short grand rice, mostly used in the preparation of sushi. Can be eaten cold, but dries out quickly if left open to air (and hard to revive once it has a dried layer).
##### Ingredients
- 86-90g rice + 154-150g water per person
##### Instructions
1. If used for sushi, make it in advance so the rice is cold to not mess up the [[_5.1.2. Seaweed|nori]].
	- Nori is delicious as long as it isn't soaking wet.
2. 3min high pressure + 10min natural release
3. optional: season with rice vinegar for sushi
4. optional: season with sesame oil for kimbap
#### Risotto rice
Usually arborio rice is used, a short-grain Italian rice with a lot of starch. The starch makes the creamy texture when cooked. It's not fluffy, so mainly used for pudding-like dishes and not for light dishes.
##### Ingredients
- 88g per person
##### Instructions
1. cook with hot stock on a high fire until done
2. wait until it has cooled down a bit before eating
#### Bomba rice
A short-grain rice, grown in Spain. It's main benefits are that it can absorb a lot of liquid and they keep their shape for a long time, which are 2 benefits sought after in the making of paëlla. 
##### Ingredients
- 90g per person
##### Instructions
1. Cook with the paella. I can't describe it on its own.

### Description
- description: Over the years, I've experimented with the big bags of rice in Asian markets, mainly the pretty 5kg and 10kg bags. The first one was originally bought to look pretty in my kitchen. I never knew there was such a difference between all rice types. It got very interesting very quickly as the type of rice can elevate or break down your dish in a heartbeat. From that moment on, I get sad for every sushi which falls apart in your hand, every risotto which doesn't get jelly, and each curry which doesn't seep into big unified blocks of rice. Though take into account that if at any point you don't have the specific type of rice, any other will do in a pinch.
