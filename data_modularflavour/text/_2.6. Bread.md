Bread is a complex pancake.

- it uses the same ingredients at its core: flour, water, and a raising agent
- it is fermented. By definition, using yeast instead of physical raising agents, you're fermenting the bread. The word fermenting in itself makes everything sound more complex.
- it can have the addition of loads of new ingredients without fighting for flavour due to the lack of (explicit) sugar.

There's the daily staple of [[_2.6.1. Simple bread|simple bread]] which I'll make twice a week. If you want to continue the bread path in complexity, we're getting to [[_2.6.2. Sourdough bread|sourdough bread]], which is a whole rollercoaster on its own.

Next to those classical breads (classical in the sense that a kid's drawing can represent it), pizza dough follows the same instructions, but is specifically created for the use of pizza. Depending on the type of oven you have, the dough will differ. In this book, [[_2.6.3. Pizza Al Taglio Bread|pizza al taglio bread]] -for the home oven- and [[_2.6.4. Pizza Napoletana Bread|pizza napoletana bread]] -for the pizza oven- are discussed.

The last one is [[_2.6.5. Hamburger Buns|hamburger buns]], which are simply put, a brioche kind of bread, but fluffied up for fatty fillings.
