The italian emulsion dishes I frequently go back to, are the ones in which I spent the most time experimenting.  They're tricky, but they feel so easy because they have ±3 ingredients only...
Each of them was made what feels like a million times before getting to a result which was decent enough to serve. There are 3 pasta dishes and one rice dish.

The first one is [[_1.3.1. Carbonara|carbonara]], which is by far the most indulgent dish of the entire book. It's not the most fatty, it's not the most unhealthy, but it just tastes like opulent luxury to me.

The second one is [[_1.3.2. Cacio e Pepe|cacio e pepe]], which is the trickiest of the dishes in the book. Though with a little trick, it's only the goodness that remains.

The third one is [[_1.3.3. Aglio e Olio|aglio e olio]], which was made famous by the movie "Chef", which makes it look so delicious. I got to know it via the youtube channel Italia Squisita.

The fourth one and only rice dish here, is [[_1.3.4. Risotto|risotto]]. It uses the same technique as cacio e pepe, but made easy due tot he slow cooking nature of rice and their amount of starch. It's the one to make when you don't want to mess up your dish.
