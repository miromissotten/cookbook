### Ingredients
- 300g firm tofu
- baste mix
	- 2tbsp soy sauce
	- 1.5tbsp honey
	- 3 cloves garlic
	- 1.5cm ginger
	- 1 pear/apple
	- 4 spring onion (only whites)
	- 2tbsp sesame oil
	- 2tsp gochujang/ssamjang
- serve
	- sesame seeds
	- spring onion


### Instructions
- tofu (removed from excess water to fry better) cut in 1cm slices
- add to bowl all the basting ingredients
- fry tofu slices in a pan for 10 minutes (until golden on both sides)
- add sauce to pan & cook for 10 minutes until reduces to glazy consistency (add water if it's too viscous)
- serve with sesame seeds & spring onion.
