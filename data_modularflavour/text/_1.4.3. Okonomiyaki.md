### Ingredients
- flour (40g per pancake)
- water (50ml/pancake)
- soba noodles (buckwheat preferred)
- soybean sprouts
- cabbage (whatever kind you want, thinly sliced)
- optional: whatever vegetables you like
- optional: Pork/bacon (2–3 slices)
- Egg (1 per pancake)
- Spring onion (raw, chopped in slices)
- nori/aonori (aonori is powdered nori)
- Okonomi sauce (or substitute with ketchup+honey+oyster sauce or bbq sauce)
- kewpie (or substitue for mayo)
- Optional: Furikake
- Katsuobushi (=Dried bonito flakes) (=dried fish flakes)

### Hardware
- 1 large frying pan (or 2 if Hiroshima style for easy layering & flipping)
- Bowl (for batter mixing)

### Instructions
- **Hiroshima style is layered** (noodles, cabbage, sprouts, ..., egg, pancake, all stacked) (prettier but way more fuss).  
- **Osaka style is mixed** (all-in-one batter, easier to make).  

#### Hiroshima Style
- Cook soba (or udon) noodles. Rinse with cold water until starch is gone (prevents sticking)
- Heat **pan 1**: stir-fry soybean sprouts (two small handfuls).
- Heat **pan 2**: stir-fry cabbage (one large handful, thinly sliced).
- Transfer cabbage (pan 2) to sprouts (pan 1) before fully done.  
  - *Optional*: add 2–3 slices of pork ribs on top.
- In **pan 2**, make a thin pancake batter:  
  - Mix 50ml water + 40g flour until crepe-like.  
  - *Optional*: add 1 tsp bouillon and/or bonito powder.  
  - Fry pancake until set.
- Transfer sprout-cabbage mix (pan 1) onto pancake (pan 2). Turn off pan 2 heat.
- In **pan 1**, fry noodles (1 portion) with soy sauce or okonomi sauce.
- Transfer noodles (pan 1) onto pancake stack (pan 2).
- In **pan 1**, fry an egg. Before fully set, transfer pancake stack (pan 2) onto egg.
- Flip everything so egg side is down in pan 1.
- Add chopped spring onion to top in pan 1. Flip once more if needed.
- Finish with toppings: okonomi sauce, mayo, dried bonito, and more spring onion.

#### Osaka Style
- Mix all Hiroshima-style ingredients together into one batter. 
- Fry into a pancake until cooked through.
- Add toppings: okonomi sauce, mayo, dried bonito, spring onion.

### Extra info
- Udon gives a chewier base, while soba gives nutty flavor. It's all up to preference.

### Description
- description: Okonomi: "what you like" ; yaki = "grilled" -> okonomiyaki = What you like, but grilled. Translation: Put everything you like in a batter, and fry it. It's quite the treat.
