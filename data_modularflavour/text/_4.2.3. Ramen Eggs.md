### Ingredients
- eggs, soft-boiled at 6min30 and shocked afterwards in an icebath for a few minutes, peeled
- water (amount varies by container size)
- soy sauce (amount varies by total weight)
- mirin (amount varies by total weight)
	- Can be replaced with 75% sake + 25% sugar mixture as mirin is at heart a simple sweet alcohol.

### Instructions
1. initial weighing
    - place the container on the scale and tare it.
    - gently add the eggs to the container
    - add water until the eggs are fully submerged
    - note the total weight of water+eggs
2. seasoning calculation
    - calculate 10% of that total weight: this is the amount of soy sauce to add
    - calculate 8% of that total weight: this is the amount of mirin to add
    - *ex: If eggs + water weigh 500g, add 50g soy sauce and 40g mirin*
3. diffusion
    - add the soy sauce & mirin in the container with the eggs&water
    - seal the container and put it in the fridge
    - wait between 24h and 4 days *(after 5 days, the texture may become soft/crumbly due to chemical breakdown; so best to eat before then)*
