### Ingredients
- 125g flour (T55)
- 5g baking powder
- 3g salt
- 1 egg
- 180g milk
- 30g butter

### Instructions
1. mix the flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl
2. add the egg & milk, and whisk until homogenous
3. put the butter in the frying pan and heat the frying pan over medium heat. once the butter is melted, pour the butter into the homogenous mixture
4. spoon some batter into the pan
5. cook until bubbles appear, flip and repeat

### Extra info
- for fluffy pancakes, add more baking soda
- for deserts, add sugar
