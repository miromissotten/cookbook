### Title
- title: Udon Noodles

### Ingredients
- 100g all-purpose flour
- 45g water
- 5g salt
- cornstarch for dusting

### Instructions
- dissolve the salt in the water
- add the salted water to the flour
- mix until a rough, shaggy dough forms
- knead firmly until the dough becomes smooth and elastic
- shape into a ball, wrap in clingfilm, and let rest for 30 minutes
- knead or gently press the dough again
- roll the dough out to 3mm thickness
- dust generously with cornstarch
- fold the dough and cut into thick, even noodles
- shake the noodles apart and dust with more starch
- boil in plenty of water for 8 (longer if thicker)
- drain and rinse under cold water
- serve
