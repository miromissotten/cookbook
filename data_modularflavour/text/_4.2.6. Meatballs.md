### Ingredients
- 1 egg
- 300g ground meat (pork + beef)
	- 50%/50%: more pork = more fatty, more beef = more complexity
- 1tbsp parsley, finely chopped
- 1tsp thyme (≈1 g)
- 1tbsp parmesan, finely grated
- 1tbsp grated onion
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1tbsp breadcrumbs
- 1tsp butter (≈5 g)
### Hardware
- mixing bowl
- skillet
### Instructions
1. In the mixing bowl, combine the ground meat, egg, parsley, thyme, parmesan, onion, garlic, and breadcrumbs
2. Shape into balls (±4 cm diameter)
3. In the skillet, heat the butter over medium heat until the butter starts to colour
4. Fry the meatballs until browned on all sides and fully cooked through (=±10 minutes)
5. Serve hot with a sauce
### Extra info
For juicier balls, put the shaped raw balls in the fridge for 25 minutes before frying
