### Ingredients
- 500g carrots
- 500g parsnips
- 500g sweet potatoes (or waxy potatoes)
- 2red onions
- 3tbsp olive oil
- 2tbsp liquid honey
- 1tbsp fresh thyme leaves (stripped from stems)
- salt
- black pepper
- any other vegetables you can think of really

### Hardware
- baking sheet

### Instructions
1. preheat oven to 200°C
2. peel & cut the carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes to 3cm pieces. 
3. cut the onions into slices
4. put everything on a baking sheet in a single layer (if the vegetables are piled on top of each other, they will steam rather than roast)
5. coat with olive oil, honey, thyme, salt, black pepper
6. roast for 20 minutes
7. flip everything around
8. roast for another 20 minutes
### Extra info
- cutting the vegetables into similar sizes is makes sure they finish cooking at the same time.
