In the world of pizza, there's an endless list of variations. Simplified in my mind, there are 2 axes to consider. 

- In terms of dough, they range from focaccia to hard taco shell.
- In terms of toppings, they range from some spices to an overload mix of vegetables and meats. 

Below, you'll see a graph on where I categorise each type. In the end, I've mainly been interested in [[_1.5.1. Pizza Al Taglio|pizza al taglio]] and [[_1.5.2. Pizza Napoletana|pizza napoletana]]. Once you've covered these 2, the others aren't as daunting anymore to take on.
