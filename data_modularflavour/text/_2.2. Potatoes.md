### Description
- description: Potatoes, what can't they do. Fluffy, hearthy, crunchy, ... it always tastes like home. Starchy will give you a fluffy potato, waxy will give you a hearthy potato, choose whichever your heart desires. Lazy potatoes require basically no work, and you get 90% of the benefits.

### Ingredients
- 400g [[_2.2. Potatoes|potatoes]]
	- the more starchy (non-waxy), the more crispy crust with fluffy inside
	- the more waxy, the more hearty and solid
- olive oil (little, but enough to coat and glisten potatoes)
- salt
- spices of choice: rosemary, thyme, paprika, cumin, black pepper, or curry-themed blends, again depending on desire
### Hardware
- large bowl    
- oven or airfryer
### Instructions
- Lazy version (25min): thin crust outside, soft and hearty inside; good pairing with salads and other rigid food
- Impress your date version (40min): thick crust outside, fluffy inside; good pairing for naughty food, or as appetizers with [[_3.4. Distinct Sauces|sauces]]
#### Lazy version
- Wash potatoes
- Cut potatoes in pieces of about 1cm cross-section, in whatever shape you prefer
- Put pieces in a big bowl & add olive oil (enough to cover without oil pooling at the bottom).
- Add spices you like (I like rosemary &|or paprika &|or thyme)
	- Optional 1: Toast spices if you like it (watch out, rosemary & thyme are delicate, cumin & black pepper can take more heat)
	- Optional 2: Temper spices by boiling them softly in semi-hot oil
		- tempering is an Indian technique, allows aromas of spices to be freed which don’t dissolve in water. As a simple rule, this works well for “curry-themed” spices.
- Put in the airfryer/oven until some spots get brown-ish (about 25 minutes at 180°C)
#### Impress-your-date version
- Wash potatoes
- Boil potatoes for about 10 minutes until they are almost done (*almost done = they should just fall off a knife when stabbed*) (will give a better crust formation and fluffier inside)
- Cut potatoes in pieces of about 1cm cross-section, in whatever shape you prefer
- Add spices you like (I like rosemary &|or paprika &|or thyme)
	- Optional 1: Toast spices if you like it (watch out, rosemary & thyme are delicate, cumin & black pepper can take more heat)
	- Optional 2: Temper spices by boiling them softly in semi-hot oil
		- tempering is an Indian technique, allows aromas of spices to be freed which don’t dissolve in water. Works well for “curry-themed” spices.
- Cover potatoes in olive oil & spices
- Put in the airfryer/oven until some spots get brown-ish (about 25 minutes at 180°C for my oven)
### Extra info
- Toss halfway through baking for even browning.
- Experiment with spice blends for different flavour profiles.
