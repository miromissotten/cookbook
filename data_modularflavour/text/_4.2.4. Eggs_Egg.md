### Instructions
#### Hard-boiled egg
- From fridge-cold: **12m** in boiling water
#### Soft-boiled egg
- From fridge-cold: **6m30s** in boiling water
#### Poached egg
- From fridge-cold: **4m** in boiling water
#### French omelet
1. whisk egg with salt & pepper.  
2. add 5g butter per egg to a non-stick pan (middle heat, this is the most difficult part to get right). 
3. scramble for 2 minutes until it starts to thicken up  
4. spread egg evenly over the pan  
5. remove from heat and tilt pan
6. fold omelet gently from top to bottom
7. tap-tap-tap the pan to slide omelet onto plate
#### Tamagoyaki
Whisked eggs, uniformly layed into a homogeneous yellow beam. Wonderful breakfast or brunch addition. 
- whisk the eggs to a uniform mixture. Add some soy sauce, water, mirin, and salt.
- in a small nonstick pan, add some oil and put over middle heat (again, most difficult part to figure out). If you have a square pan, it's really pretty. otherwise it gets a bit disappointing
- add a very thin layer of egg to cover the pan
- when the thin layer is solid at the bottom but still slightly runny at the top, roll it up to the side of the pan
- continue adding layers and rolling on top of the previous layered roll until done
