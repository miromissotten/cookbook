### Title
- title: Fresh Pasta

### Ingredients
- 100g tipo 00 flour
- 1 egg (or more egg yolk to egg white ratio for a sweeter taste)
### Instructions
- mix egg & flour together
- shape into a ball & wrap in clingfilm
- refrigerate for 25 minutes
- shape into the desired pasta shape (spaghetti, linguini, orechiette, ...)
- boil for 1-2 minutes
- drain
