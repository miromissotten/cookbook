### Title
- title: Miso Tare

### Ingredients
- 140g (1) raw onion pureed in a food processor, or grated finely
- 7g (±2 cloves) garlic, grated
- 6g ginger, grated (or 0.6g dried ginger)
- 400g white miso
- 100g red miso
- 20g mirin (or 15g sake + 5g sugar)
- 30g soy sauce
- 5g sesame oil
- 14g tahini
- 1g black pepper (±20 grinds from a pepper mill)
- 1g gochujang
- 1g ssamyang
- pinch of togarashi (or cayenne)

### Instructions
- add half of the raw onion puree to a small pan.
- fry over low heat, stirring frequently, until golden brown, around 20 minutes. take care that it doesn't burn, which sounds easier than it is.
- place the fried onion in a mixing bowl.
- when the onion has cooled, add all remaining other ingredients
- Place in the fridge in a covered container and allow the tare to mature for at least 24 hours to better develop the flavour and remove some of the harshness from the raw vegetables. The tare will keep for up to 12 months.
- Add 35g of tare per 300g soup.
