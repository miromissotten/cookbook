### Ingredients
- 1 whole head garlic, broken into individual peeled cloves (45-60g)
- fresh juice from 3 to 4 lemons (160 ml)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin (2 g)
- 1 generous cup tahini paste (about 10 ounces; 300 g by weight)
- Water
- Salt
### Instructions
1. blend garlic and lemon juice until smooth
	- *Note: Puréeing garlic directly in lemon juice prevents sharp, pungent compounds (allicin) from forming due to the low pH. This gives a smooth, sweet garlic flavor without the burn.*
	    - ![[nerdinfo_allinase.png|300]]
2. Add cumin and tahini paste to the blended lemon/garlic juice and whisk to combine.
	1. The mixture will seize up and turn pasty. Add water a few tablespoons at a time until you can almost make a ribbon (but not quite).
3. Season to taste with salt. Refrigerate for up to 1.5 weeks.
