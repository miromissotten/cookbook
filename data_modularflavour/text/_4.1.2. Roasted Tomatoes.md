
### Title
- title: Roasted Tomatoes

### Description
- description: There's multiple ways you can treat tomatoes, but below I singled out my 2 favourite ones which make them feel special.
### Ingredients
- Tomatoes (could be either cherry or regular. I prefer regular, but can see the appeal of little tomatoes as a singular solid component in a dish.)
	- 150g/p = side dish
	- 210g/p = part of main dish
	- 300g/p = main dish centerpiece)
- olive oil
- salt
- garlic
### Instructions

#### Slow Roast

goal: be a lot. They become jammy, sweet, intensely savory

- works best with halve ripe tomatoes as they tend to lose all structure if they're already fully ripened.
- add salt, olive oil, and a little garlic.
- roast at 150°C for 2-3h.
- serve with basil, olive oil and a little splash of vinegar

#### Char

goal: add umami. Adds a lot of contrast within one ingredient

- cook tomatoes under a very hot grill/oven/airfryer/over a flame until deeply blistered.
- keep some raw interior while getting serious char on the skin
- serve with salt and olive oil
- works great as the center of the dish

#### Cheat

goal: make them more interesting than the raw component

- coat in oil, and fry them face down in a pan
- when they start to take on some colour, take them off
- add some soy sauce and sugar in the pan
- put them again face down in the soy sauce+sugar mixture for another 5 mins, just before they start to burn. (risky, because sugar tends to burn quickly)
