### Ingredients
- 225 g dried chickpeas
    - *Note: Dried chickpeas provide significantly better flavor and texture than canned ones.*
- 2x 6g baking soda
    - *Note: Baking soda raises the water's pH. This helps chickpea skins break down and soften efficiently.*
- 24g + 12g salt
- 100 g onion, halved
- 40 g celery stalk
- 50 g carrot
- 2 medium cloves garlic (8g)
- 2 bay leaves
- 280g [[_3.4.3.5. Tahini sauce with garlic and lemon|tahini sauce with garlic and lemon]]
- Optional: Za'atar/Ras El Hanout, paprika, warmed whole chickpeas, chopped fresh parsley leaves, ... for serving

### Instructions
1. Combine the chickpeas, 6g baking soda, 24g salt , and 1.4l cold water in a large bowl. Let stand at room temperature overnight. Drain and rinse the beans thoroughly.
2. create [[_3.4.3.5. Tahini sauce with garlic and lemon|tahini sauce with garlic and lemon]].
3. Place the beans in a large pot. Add the 6g baking soda, 12g salt, onion, celery, carrot, garlic, and bay leaves. Add 1.4 L water and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to a simmer and cover 90% with a lid. Cook until the beans are completely tender and falling apart (about 2 hours)
	- Check occasionally and add water if needed to keep the beans submerged.
    - *Overcooking the chickpeas until they fall apart makes them easy to blend into a smooth texture without needing to peel them.*
    - *Cooking with aromatics like onion, celery, and carrot adds flavor to the chickpeas from the inside out.*
4. Discard the onion, celery, and bay leaves
5. Blend the chickpeas, carrot, and garlic with 180 ml of the cooking liquid. 
    - *Why it works: Blending the cooked carrot and garlic adds savory complexity without overpowering the chickpea flavor.*
6. Start blending on the lowest speed and slowly increase to high. (If the mixture is too thick, add some more cooking liquid). Blend until completely smooth (about 2 minutes).
	- Reserve 200 ml of the remaining cooking liquid in a container and refrigerate. if the hummus hardens too solidly, you can blend some more in later.
    - *Blending while hot is the secret to smoothness. Starchy mixtures become pasty as they cool. Keeping them hot allows them to circulate properly in the blender.*
7. Whisk in the [[_3.4.3.5. Tahini sauce with garlic and lemon|tahini sauce with garlic and lemon]]. Whisk in salt to taste. Transfer to a sealed container and cool to room temperature. It will thicken until it holds its shape. If the purée is too thick, whisk in the reserved cooking liquid, 15 ml at a time, until the consistency is right.
8. Serve with desired additions
9. Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 1 week
