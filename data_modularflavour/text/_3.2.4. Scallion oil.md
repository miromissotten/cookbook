### Description
- description: A simple composition of a flavourful kitchen aid. It can work for any dish I've encountered so far, unlike the other aromatic oils which can overpower quickly.

### Ingredients
- 125ml neutral oil (vegetable)
- 60-80g scallion whites (no ends, no dark green)

### Instructions
- in a small saucepan, add the oil and scallions.
- cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally until the scallions look light golden. (Takes around 10-15 minutes.)
- remove from the heat. reserve in the pot and allow it to cool
- strain and transfer oil to a melt-resistant contains. if not used immediately, store in the fridge for up to 6 months.
