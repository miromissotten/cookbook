### Text
Seaweed exists in many shapes and forms. Like grass, there's tiny little ones, thick spiky ones, long broad ones, etc. Below are the types usually used in cooking. 
#### Nori

- = roasted thin sheets of seaweed
- flavour: mild-to-strong umami
- uses: wrapping or garnish
- other: nori absorbs moisture very easily. store airtight.
#### Kombu
- = thick, leather-like dried kelp
- flavour: very rich in glutamates (to create umami)
- uses: dashi
- other: not eaten as a sheet like nori

#### Wakame
- = thin slightly chewy seaweed. Can be strips or thicked leafs
- flavour: in between water and lettuce
- uses: miso soup and salads

#### Hijiki
- = small dark thin strips of seaweed
- flavour: wakame with an earthy/nutty touch
- uses: Japanese side dishes
