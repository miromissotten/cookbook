### Description
- description: Miso = Fermented soybean paste. The main ingredients making up miso are soybeans, salt, and koji (a cultured mold, usually on rice or barley).

### Text
- ⚪ Shiro Miso (White Miso): 
	- Short fermentation
	- Mildest, sweetest, lowest salt
	- High koji content
- 🟡 Shinshu Miso (Yellow Miso): 
	- Medium fermentation
	- All-purpose, balanced, earthy
- 🔴 Aka Miso (Red Miso): 
	- Long fermentation
	- Strongest, richest, saltiest
	- High soybean content
