### Text
Miso is fermented soybean paste. The main ingredients making up miso are soybeans, salt, and koji (a cultured mold, usually on rice or barley). There are multiple types, which are categorised based on the duration of the fermentation.

#### ⚪ Shiro Miso (White Miso): 
- Short fermentation
- Mildest, sweetest, lowest salt
- High koji content

#### 🟡 Shinshu Miso (Yellow Miso): 
- Medium fermentation
- All-purpose, balanced, earth

#### 🔴 Aka Miso (Red Miso): 
- Long fermentation
- Strongest, richest, saltiest
- High soybean content
