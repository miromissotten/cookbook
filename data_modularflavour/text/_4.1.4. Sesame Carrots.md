### Ingredients
- 2 carrots
- sesame oil
- olive oil
- soy sauce
- sesame seeds

### Instructions
- shape the carrots how you like
	- ribbons is my preferred because it looks fancy (slice with a peeler)
	- rounds/diagonal cuts/battonets/... anything goes
- coat the carrots in 90% olive oil and 10% sesame oil (sesame oil is way stronger in flavour), and add a tsp of soy sauce
- fry in a pan until the carrots start to char (to dark brown, but not yet burned!)
- if the carrots start to char before they're the texture you'd like, add some water in the pan.
- when done, add some sesame seeds to your liking
### Description
- description: Carrots are boring on themselves, and moreover I'm allergic if they're raw... So, frying them in a little sesame oil makes them more interesting and add a beautiful orange colour to your dish (which is the only reason I add them honestly).
