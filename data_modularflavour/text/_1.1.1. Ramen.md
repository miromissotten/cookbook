### Hardware
- pretty ramen bowls
### Instructions
- in a serving bowl, add the tare
- pour the hot bouillon over the top and stir until everything is dissolved and combined
- add your freshly cooked ramen noodles
- add the aromatic oil
- arrange the desired toppings on top. 
- serve immediately
