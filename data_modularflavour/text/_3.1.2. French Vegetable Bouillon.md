### Ingredients
- 250g carrots (±3-4)
- 250g leek (±2)
- 150g celery stalks (±3)
- 150g celeriac (±0.5 small celeriac)
- 150g turnip (±1-2)
- 150g onion (±1-2)
- 10g garlic (±2 cloves)
- 2.5l cold water
- 8 black peppercorns (whole, not ground)
- 2 bay leaves
- 1tsp thyme
- 1tsp parsley
- 10g salt (adjust to taste)
- 10g butter/oil (optional, for sweating the vegetables)
### Instructions
- chop the vegetables. There is no need to make the cuts pretty because everything will be strained out later.
- fry the vegetables in butter. Sweat gently for 8 minutes without browning them. The more you brown them (without burning, the sweeter and darker the end result will be)
- add the remaining ingredients.
- add 2.5L cold water. Slowly bring everything to a gentle simmer.
- simmer gently for 01h00-01h15. Do not let it boil aggressively, as this can make the bouillon cloudy and give it a muddier flavor.
- strain the bouillon. Don't press the vegetables if you want a particularly clear bouillon, otherwise do as it contains lots of flavour.
- add salt to taste.
