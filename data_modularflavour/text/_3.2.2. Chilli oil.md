### Instructions
- put in pot 1
	- 20 g coarse chilli flakes
	- 8 g fine chilli powder
	- 8 g sesame seeds
	- 2 g salt
	- 2 g sugar
	- 1 g MSG
- in pot 2, heat 120 ml neutral oil with
	- 1 star anise
	- small piece of cinnamon
	- a little dried ginger
	- sichuan peppercorns
- heat for 10-15 minutes at roughly 120-140°C (simmering, NOT a lot of airbubbles)
- strain out the aromatics from pot 2
- heat pot 2 with the oil to about 170–185°C (high temperature, lots of air bubbles)
- remove from heat, then pour ⅔ of the oil over the chilli mixture in pot 1 and stir
- wait 20-30 seconds, then add the remaining oil
- Let it cool before transferring to a clean jar.

- 2cm cinnamon stick
- 1tsp dried ginger (dry for longer shelf life)
- 2g salt
- 2g sugar
- 1g MSG
- 8g sesame seeds
