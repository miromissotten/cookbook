### Title
- title: Cacio e Pepe

### Hardware
- microwave/heating supply
- fork
- pot

### Ingredients
- 40g pecorino
	- or replace with a mix of hard cheeses, but pecorino's harshness is pretty welcome in the simplicity of the dish
- 1g black pepper
- 100g pasta
- 2g corn flour/starch

### Instructions
There is the original version, but getting it right is honestly too much of a bother to me. I prefer a version with a big cheat which barely ever fails and allows for a slightly higher temperature of serving. Below is thus this cheat version.
- start cooking the pasta in salted water
- mix 20ml water + 2g of corn flour/starch
- put the mixture into the microwave for 20s and let it cool down for 2minutes after that. 
- grate the cheese(s)
- add the cheese with a drop of oil to the mixture and mix it all (with blender or just intensely with a fork). 
- when the pasta is al dente, take it out. 
- when the pasta has cooled down to the point of barely able to put a flat hand on the pasta without burning, add the pasta to the sauce mixture. 
- add black pepper and pecorino as garnish at the table
