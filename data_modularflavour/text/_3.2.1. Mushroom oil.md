### Ingredients
- 120ml neutral oil
- 14g dried shiitake
- 14g dried porcini
- 6 spring onions
- (optional: a bit of truffle oil if desired, but watch out as it's very overpowering quickly)
### Hardware
- saucepan
### Instructions
- add oil, dried shiitake, dried porcini, and spring onions to a saucepan.
- heat gently on the stove until it begins to bubble.
- remove from the heat and let the ingredients infuse into the oil as it cools.
