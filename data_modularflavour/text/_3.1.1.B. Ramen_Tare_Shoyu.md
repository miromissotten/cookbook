### Ingredients
- 170g fresh Shiitake (or 17g of dried)
- 25g (15cm) Kombu
- 40g ginger
- 75g (±6) spring onions
- 50g (±6) garlic cloves
- 120g soy sauce
- 120g mirin (or 80g sake+40g sugar or 50g whisky+20g sugar)
### Hardware
- saucepan
### Instructions
- combine all ingredients in a saucepan
- boil the mixture for 20-30 minutes, then strain
- can be kept in the freezer, though soy sauce doesn't freeze well due to its high salt content (which lowers the freezing point which your standard freezer barely reaches)
- serve by using 20-30g per 300g broth
