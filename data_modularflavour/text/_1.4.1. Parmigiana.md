### Ingredients
- 800g (±2) eggplants
- 500ml [[_3.4.1.1. Tomato Sauce|tomato sauce]]
- 250g mozzarella
- 80g parmigiano
- 100g all-purpose flour
- optional: 100g breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp dried oregano (or fresh basil)
- olive oil
- salt
- black pepper

### Instructions
#### preparation:
- In general: prepare the ingredients
- aubergine
	- cut in long thin slices
	- dry by sprinkling with salt, waiting for about 30min, and then removing the excessive moisture with kitchen paper
	- cover in mixture of 100g flour, 4g salt, 0.5g pepper
		- (or more with same proportions)
	- fry in a pan or bake in an oven until light brown. (usually, I like baking better)
- [[_3.4.1.1. Tomato Sauce|tomato sauce]]
- mozzarella
	- Squeeze it to dry out the mozzarella. 

#### assembly:
- optioneel: beetje olijfolie over sprenkelen
- broodkruim & oregano & parmesaan voor topping

#### baking:
- 35min in 180°C covered with aluminium foil
- 10min in 180°C without aluminium foil
