### Ingredients
- the to-be-pickled-vegetable
	- 1 large red onion
	- red babbage
	- cucumbers
	- radish
	- ...probably more, but I didn't test others
- 180ml white vinegar
- 120ml hot water
- 8.5g sugar (2 tsp)
- 6g salt (1 tsp)
- optional: 2 cloves garlic (slices), 5 whole peppercorns
### Hardware
- mason jar (or other glass container with a lid)
- mandoline or a sharp knife
### Instructions
1. thinly slice the to-be-pickled-vegetable and place the slices into a mason jar
	1. For a milder flavour, you can pour boiling water over the sliced to-be-pickled-vegetable in a strainer before adding them to the jar
2. in a measuring cup, combine the hot water, vinegar, sugar, and salt
3. stir until the sugar and salt have completely dissolved
4. pour the vinegar mixture over the to-be-pickled-vegetable in the jar until fully submerged
5. wait at room temperature for at least 00h30
6. seal the jar
7. can be kept up to three weeks in the fridge
### Extra info
There are many possible variations. You can experiment with different types of vinegar (ex. apple/wine vinegar) or add extra flavours to the brine (ex. red pepper flakes for heat/herbs like thyme or dill for herbal freshness)
