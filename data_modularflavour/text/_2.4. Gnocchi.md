### Ingredients
- 750g floury [[_2.2. Potatoes|potatoes]]
- 150g [[_5.1.7. Flour|flour]] (T55)
- 5g salt
- 20g [[_5.1.7. Flour|flour]] for dusting

### Instructions
1. preheat the oven to 200°C
2. wash the potatoes and bake them whole for 50-60 minutes (pricking with a fork doesn't pick them up anymore)
3. cool the potatoes for 10 minutes until cool enough to touch
4. skin the potato peel from the potatoes. This should be easy when they're still warm.
5. mash the potatoes as fine as possible
6. spread the mashed potatoes out and let it cool for 2-3 minutes
7. add 150g flour and 5g salt
8. knead together for 1-2 minutes, but do not overwork it
9. If they fall apart or feel too wet, add more flour.
10. create multiple cilinders of 2cm diameter
11. cut each cilinder into 2cm pieces
12. dust the gnocchi lightly with flour
13. optional: roll each piece over a fork to create ridges and make them look cool
14. cook the gnocchi in heavily salted water. When they float to the surface (after 2-3 minutes), they're done. Remove them 30 seconds after.
15. Now, you can serve them, or fry them in a hot pan for a few minutes a more beautiful golden look.

### Extra info
- At all cost, try to minimise the amount of flour used.
- use starchy potatoes rather than waxy potatoes. Starchy ones have
	- less moisture -> less flour needed -> less heavy/chewy gnocchi, more tender/fluffy gnocchi
	- more starch -> "gelatenise" water -> more cohesion
- baking the potatoes instead of boiling them keeps the dough drier and reduces the amount of flour needed
- if freezing, it's best to freeze them in a single layer first for a few hours before putting them in boxes because otherwise they tend to stick together.
- if cooking gnocchi from frozen; do not thaw first, just add a minute of boiling time
