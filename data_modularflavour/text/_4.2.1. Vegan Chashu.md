### Ingredients
- 1 block firm tofu
- **Marinade Mix:**
	- 60g (4 tbsp) soy sauce
	- 36g (2 tbsp) mirin
	- 15g (1 tbsp) rice vinegar
	- 4.5g (1 tsp) neutral vegetable oil
	- 9g (3 cloves) garlic, minced
	- 2g (1 tsp) ginger, minced
- **Basting Mix:**
    - 60g (4 tbsp) soy sauce
    - Sugar/honey/syrup
    - 9g (2 tsp) neutral oil (rapeseed for ex)
### Hardware
- airfryer
- little sauce-pots 
- heavy pot or tofu press
- oven/airfryer
### Instructions
- Wrap the firm tofu in kitchen paper and press with a heavy pot on top for 30 minutes to remove excess water
- Slice the pressed tofu into 0.5cm thick slices
- Mix the marinade ingredients and apply evenly to the tofu slices.
- Bake in the oven for 15 minutes, flip the slices, and bake for another 15 minutes.
- Allow to cool and then refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
- mix the basting ingredients. Cyclically baste the tofu with the mixture and heat it in an airfryer or pan until glazed and heated through.
### Extra info
- The vegan chashu develops a firmer, more meat-like texture after freezing before preparing it.
