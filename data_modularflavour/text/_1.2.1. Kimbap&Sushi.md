### Description
- description: Kimbap = rice seasoned with sesame oil (looks like maki) pretty much anything you want (grilled meat, any sort of cabbage, carrot, kimchi, eggs, ...) ; Sushi = rice with vinegar and raw fish, seafood, raw vegetables. Since I like kimbap way more, I'll only describe kimbap here, but sushi is basically the same, but with vinegar and raw food.

### Hardware
- Bamboo rolling mat
- Knife
- Rice cooker/instant pot or pot
### Instructions
- Place nori on bamboo mat, (shiny side down)
- Spread rice evenly.
- Put fillings on rice.
- Roll tightly with bamboo mat
- Slice into pieces.
