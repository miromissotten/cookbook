### Instructions
There's 2 main types of soft shell tortillas, but there's a time and place for everything. Corn tortillas aren't flexible because they don't have gluten which stretch. Thus they're best used for fixed tacos. They also add a little "earthy" flavour, more interesting to me. If you want to make a burrito or wrap something, only regular tortillas will work and the corn ones will just break.

#### Regular Tortillas
##### Ingredients
- 250 g flour
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil (or softened butter)
- 150 ml warm water
##### Instructions
- mix flour, salt, and baking powder
- add the oil and rub it into the flour
- slowly add warm water while mixing until you have a soft dough like play-doh
- knead 2 minutes until smooth
- cover and let rest 25 minutes
- divide into 8 balls
- flatten the balls to thin, 20cm wide discs
- heat a dry frying pan
- cook each tortilla for about 40 seconds per side. the side is done when you see a few brown spots
- finished tortillas can be wrapped in a clean towel to stay soft
#### Corn Tortillas
Warning: Regular corn flour from a supermarket won't work. Look for “masa harina” explicitly!
##### Ingredients
- 200 g masa harina
- ½ tsp salt
- 150–170 ml warm water
##### Instructions
- mix masa harina + salt.
- same instructions as for regular tortillas to continue (but keep in mind these aren't stretchy and can break)

### Extra info
- If they turn out hard, you probably cooked them too long or rolled them too thick. Keep the cooking time short.
