### Ingredients
- 250 ml beef stock / demi-glace
- 250 ml red wine (dry preferred, otherwise sauce too sweet)
- 1 shallot
- 1 tsp dried thyme (=0.5g)   
- 6–8 black peppercorns, lightly crushed
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 55 g unsalted butter, in 1cm³ pieces (cold)
- Optional (flavour): 1 bay leaf
- Optional (texture): a splash (10–15 ml) of heavy cream for silkiness
### Hardware
- saucepan 1 (small)
- skillet 1
### Instructions
1. In saucepan 1, combine the wine, thyme, bay leaf, and peppercorns. -> Reduce volume to 1/2.
2. In saucepan 1, Add beef stock (or demi-glace) -> reduce to 1/3
3. In skillet 1, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the shallot until softened and lightly golden.
4. Strain saucepan 1 into skillet 1. -> discard the strained out herbs and peppercorns.
5. Lower the heat, then whisk in the cold butter one piece at a time until the sauce thickens and develops a glossy finish.
6. Optional: add heavy cream just before serving (to avoid clotting)
### Extra info
Ideally, use a pan that has fond (browned bits from searing meat) and deglaze it with the wine at step 1.
