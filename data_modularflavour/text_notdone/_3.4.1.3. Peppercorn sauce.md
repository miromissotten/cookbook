### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil (or butter)
- 1 small shallot, finely minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp green peppercorns (in brine, drained and rinsed)
- 1 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 1/2 cup beef broth (or vegetable broth)
- 1/4 cup brandy or cognac (optional)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh parsley or chives, chopped (for garnish, optional)

### Hardware
- Medium saucepan or skillet
- Whisk
- Mortar and pestle or rolling pin (for crushing peppercorns)

### Instructions
- Heat oil in saucepan over medium heat.
- Add minced shallot and cook until softened (3-4 min).
- Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant (1 min).
- Stir in green and crushed black peppercorns; cook (1 min).
- If using, add brandy/cognac; simmer to reduce (1-2 min).
- Pour in beef broth; simmer to reduce by half (3-5 min).
- Reduce heat to low. Stir in heavy cream, Dijon mustard, and Worcestershire sauce.
- Gently simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened (5-7 min).
- Taste and season with salt as needed.
- Serve hot, garnished with parsley or chives if desired.

### Extra info
- Freshly crushing black peppercorns significantly enhances flavor.
- Green peppercorns offer a milder, aromatic note.
- Adjust sauce consistency by adding more broth/cream (thinner) or simmering longer (thicker).
- Sauce can be made a day ahead; reheat gently on low heat, adding liquid if too thick.
- For a richer sauce, add pan drippings from cooked meat.
- For a spicier kick, add a pinch of red pepper flakes.
