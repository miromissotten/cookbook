### Ingredients
- salad 
	- 4 heads of mixed lettuce (any lettuce type you like)
	- 2 large cucumbers, thinly sliced
	- 500 g cherry tomatoes
	- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
	- 3-4 medium carrots, shredded
	- 2 bell peppers, thinly sliced (any colour)
- lemon vinaigrette 
	- 240 ml extra virgin olive oil
	- 120 ml lemon juice
	- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
	- optional: 2 cloves garlic (minced)
	- 1 tsp salt
	- 1/2 tsp black pepper
### Hardware
- large salad bowl
- salad spinner (highly recommended to dry the lettuce and avoid a soggy salad)
- small bowl
- whisk/fork
### Instructions
- prepare the vegetables
    - wash lettuce
    - dry the leaves completely using a salad spinner
    - if preparing in advance, store the dry leaves in a sealed bag or container in the refrigerator with a paper towel to absorb moisture.
    - cut up all the other vegetables
- create the vinaigrette
    - in a small bowl, combine the lemon juice, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
    - slowly whisk in the olive oil until the dressing is emulsified.
- assemble
    - combine everything in a bowl
    - serve the vinaigrette on the side because nobody likes soggy salad
### Extra info
- never dress the salad in advance.
