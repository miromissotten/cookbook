### Ingredients
- 100 g unsalted butter
- 1 large clove garlic, crushed and finely chopped
- 13 g coarsely chopped fresh sage leaves
- 0.6 g ground black pepper (or to taste)
- Fine salt, to taste
### Hardware
- Medium saucepan (light-colored recommended to monitor browning)
- Stirring utensil
### Instructions
- In the saucepan, melt the 113 g of unsalted butter over medium-low heat.
- Once the butter begins to bubble, add the crushed and finely chopped garlic.
- Stir the mixture for about 1 minute until it becomes fragrant.
- Add the 17 g of chopped fresh sage leaves.
- Continue stirring for 2 to 4 minutes until the butter turns a light brown color and gives off a nutty aroma.
- Season with 0.6 g of black pepper and salt to your liking.
- Serve immediately.
### Extra info
- This sauce is known as *beurre noisette* in French and *burro e salvia* in Italian. It is a classic pairing for pasta dishes, especially filled pastas like ravioli and gnocchi, in Northern Italy.
- Be careful not to let the butter burn; there is a fine line between browned and burnt.
- The browned milk solids at the bottom of the pan hold most of the flavor, so be sure to scrape them up and include them in the sauce.
- This sauce is versatile and can be served over vegetables, fish, chicken, or pasta.
