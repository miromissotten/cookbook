
### Title
- title: Spicy Tofu Crumble

### Ingredients
- 400g firm tofu
- baste mix
	- 1tbsp neutral oil
	- 2tbsp soy sauce
	- 1tbsp gochujang
	- 1tsp [[_3.2.2. Chilli oil|chilli oil]]
	- 1tsp cayenne pepper
	- 1tbsp tomato paste
	- 1tsp smoked paprika
	- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
	- 0.5tsp cumin
	- 1tsp brown sugar
	- 1tsp lime juice (or rice vinegar if in a pinch)

### Instructions
- crumble the tofu into pieces until it looks like minced meat
- mix all basting ingredients
- fry the tofu crumble in the baste mix. If it doesn't get crunchy enough to your taste, you can finish it off in the oven/airfryer
