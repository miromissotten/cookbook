### Ingredients
- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
- 180 ml white vinegar
- 120 ml hot water
- 8.5 g sugar (approximately 2 tsp)
- 6 g salt (approximately 1 tsp) [2]
- Optional (flavour): 2 cloves garlic (sliced), a few whole peppercorns, a bay leaf
### Hardware
- Mason jar or other glass container with a lid
- Mandoline or a sharp knife
- Measuring cup
### Instructions
1.  Thinly slice the red onion and place the slices into a mason jar. For a milder onion flavor, you can pour boiling water over the sliced onions in a strainer before adding them to the jar.
2.  In a measuring cup, combine the hot water, vinegar, sugar, and salt. Stir until the sugar and salt have completely dissolved.
3.  Pour the vinegar mixture over the onions in the jar, ensuring they are fully submerged.
4.  Let the jar sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes. The onions are ready when they turn bright pink and are tender.
5.  Seal the jar and store it in the refrigerator for up to three weeks.
### Extra info
There are many possible variations for this recipe. You can experiment with different types of vinegar like apple cider or white wine vinegar. You can also add other flavorings to the brine, such as red pepper flakes for heat, or fresh herbs like thyme or dill.
