### Ingredients
- 165g brussels sprouts
- neutral oil (e.g., avocado or grapeseed)
- salt
- black pepper
- miso glaze:
    - 10g [[_5.1.1. Miso|miso]] paste (shiro or shinshu)
    - 7g honey (or maple syrup if vegan)
    - 4g rice vinegar
    - 5g [[_5.1.3. Soy Sauce|soy sauce]]
    - 2g toasted sesame oil
    - 1g fresh ginger
    - 3g (1 clove) garlic
- Optional: sesame seeds
- Optional: 1 scallion, thinly sliced

### Hardware
- 1 medium mixing bowl

### Instructions
- Cut each sprout in half from top to bottom (slice top to bottom to keep the leaves attached or your cutting board becomes a graveyard of loose-leaf-corpses)
- In the medium mixing bowl, coat the halved sprouts in oil
- Spread the sprouts in a single layer
	- if not roasted in a single layer, the sprouts will steam and become soft instead of crispy
	- if oven: place them cut-side down for the best caramelization.
	- if airfryer: orientation doesn't matter
- put in the oven for 15-20 minutes @220°C, until they are starting to get golden (not yet dark brown as they'll have to return to the oven later with the glaze)
- in the same mixing bowl, mix the miso glaze ingredients together until homogenous
- Remove the sprouts from the oven and coat them with the miso glaze
- put in the oven for 5 minutes, or until the glaze is bubbly and caramelized
