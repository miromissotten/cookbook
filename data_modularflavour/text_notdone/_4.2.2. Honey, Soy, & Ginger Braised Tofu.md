### Instructions
- 280g firm tofu (removed from excess water to fry better) cut in 1cm slices
- add to bowl:
	- 2 tbsp soy sauce
	- 1.5 tbsp honey
	- 3 cloves garlic
	- 1.5cm ginger
	- 1 pear
	- 4 spring onion whites
	- 2tbsp sesame oil
	- 1.5 tsp gochujang
	- 1tbsp water
- fry tofu slices in a pan for 8 minutes (until golden on both sides)
- add sauce to pan & cook for 10 minutes until reduces to glazy consistency
- serve with sesame seeds & spring onion.
