### Ingredients
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1-2 tsp smoked paprika (use this, not sweet paprika, for the smoky flavor)
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (or more, to taste for spiciness)
- 1/2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- Optional: 1 tsp tomato paste (to add a little depth like adobo)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Hardware
- Mixing bowl
- Whisk or spoon

### Instructions
- In the mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise and fresh lemon juice.
- Add the smoked paprika, cayenne pepper, sweet paprika, and ground cumin. If using, add the tomato paste as well.
- Whisk all the ingredients together until the mixture is smooth and evenly colored.
- Season with salt and pepper to taste, and stir again.
- For the best flavor, cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving. This allows the smoky and spicy flavors to meld with the mayonnaise.

### Extra info
- The key ingredient here is **smoked paprika**. It is essential for mimicking the smoky flavor of chipotle peppers. Regular or sweet paprika will not provide the same result.
- This recipe is a convenient alternative when you don't have chipotle peppers in adobo. You can easily adjust the smokiness and heat by changing the amounts of smoked paprika and cayenne pepper.
- Like the chipotle version, this aioli is a versatile condiment. It's excellent as a dipping sauce for fries and vegetables or as a flavorful spread on burgers, sandwiches, and wraps.
