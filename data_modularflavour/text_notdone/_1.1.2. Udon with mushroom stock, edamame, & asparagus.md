### Hardware
- pot

### Ingredients
- soup
	- 500g mushrooms
	- 1 onion
	- 4 cloves of garlic
	- 2cm ginger
	- 6tbsp miso
	- 1tbsp tahini
	- 2tbsp neutral oil
	- 3tbsp soy sauce
	- 1l soy milk
- non-soup
	- 250g hard tofu
	- 250g asparagus
	- 100g edamame
	- 130g [[_2.5.3. Udon Noodles|udon noodles]]

### Instructions
- assemble all ingredients
	- -> blend all ingredients for the soup
- Once silky smooth, bake in pan for 15 minutes (continuously stirring to avoid burning the paste)
- Add soy milk little by little (but don’t let it heat too much because otherwise the milk curdles).
- Bake/fry asparagus & edamame (good with some chilli flakes)
	- -> set aside
- Make udon according to package
- Put chilli, garlic, salt & lemon juice in a pan for 1 minute
	- -> set aside
- Fry tofu with soy sauce
	- -> assemble all in a bowl
