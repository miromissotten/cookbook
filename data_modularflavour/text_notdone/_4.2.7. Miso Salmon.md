### Dietary Restrictions
- Lactosefree: no
- Vegetarian: no
- Vegan: no
- Glutenfree: yes

### Title
- title: Miso Salmon

### Ingredients
- 1kg salmon
- baste mix
	- 90g white miso (about 5 tbsp)
	- 25g sake
	- 25g mirin
- serve with
	- sesame seeds
	- spring onions

### Instructions
- mix baste mix ingredients
- put salmon in a bag/well-fitting container and add the baste mix
- wait 1-2 days in fridge (salmon in fridge, not you)
- remove excess marinade (without water)
- fry salmon, skin side down, in little oil and on a low fire for about 3 min on skin, 3 min other side, then put it on a plate/tray/board
- wait 5 mins (like a steak, it needs to rest while it continues cooking)
- serve with sesame seeds & spring onion
