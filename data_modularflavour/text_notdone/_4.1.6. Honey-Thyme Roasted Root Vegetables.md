### Ingredients
- 500g Carrots
- 500g Parsnips
- 500g Sweet potatoes (or waxy potatoes)
- 2 Red onions
- 3 tbsp Olive oil
- 2 tbsp Liquid honey
- 1 tbsp Fresh thyme leaves (stripped from stems)
- Salt
- Black pepper

### Hardware
- Large baking sheet (or two)
- Chef's knife
- Cutting board
- Vegetable peeler
- Large mixing bowl
- Spatula

### Instructions
1.  **Preheat**: Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
2.  **Prep Vegetables**: Peel the carrots, parsnips, and sweet potatoes. Cut them into uniform chunks (approx. 3-4 cm). Peel the onions and cut them into wedges.
3.  **Season**: Place all the vegetables in a large mixing bowl. Drizzle with the olive oil and honey. Sprinkle with the fresh thyme, a generous pinch of salt, and cracked black pepper. Toss well to coat everything evenly.
4.  **Arrange**: Spread the vegetables out on the baking sheet in a single layer. **Note**: Do not overcrowd the pan; if the vegetables are piled on top of each other, they will steam rather than roast. Use two trays if necessary.
5.  **Roast**: Place in the oven and roast for 35 to 45 minutes.
6.  **Flip**: Halfway through the cooking time (around the 20-minute mark), remove the tray and toss/flip the vegetables with a spatula to ensure even browning.
7.  **Serve**: Remove when the vegetables are tender (pierce easily with a fork) and the edges are caramelized and golden brown. Serve hot.

### Extra info
- **Uniformity**: Cutting the vegetables into similar sizes is crucial so they finish cooking at the same time.
- **Variations**: You can add beetroot for color, but be aware it may stain the other vegetables pink. Butternut squash is also a great addition.
- **Crispiness**: If you want them crispier, increase heat to 220°C (425°F) for the last 5-10 minutes, keeping a close eye on the honey to prevent burning.
