### Instructions
#### Hard-boiled egg
- From fridge-cold: **12m** in boiling water
#### Soft-boiled egg
- From fridge-cold: **6m30s** in boiling water
#### Poached egg
- From fridge-cold: **4m** in boiling water
#### French omelet
1. whisk egg with salt & pepper.  
2. add 5g butter per egg to a non-stick pan (middle heat, this is the most difficult part to get right). 
3. scramble for 2 minutes until it starts to thicken up  
4. spread egg evenly over the pan  
5. remove from heat and tilt pan
6. fold omelet gently from top to bottom
7. tap-tap-tap the pan to slide omelet onto plate
#### Tamagoyaki
Whisked eggs, uniformly layed into a homogeneous yellow beam. Wonderful breakfast or brunch addition.
