### Ingredients
- 1l [[_3.1.3. Quick Japanese Broth|quick Japanese broth]]
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 4 scallions, chopped
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 85 g vegan kimchi, chopped
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 450 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 454 g soft or silken tofu, cut in large chunks
- 150 g enoki mushrooms
- 1 tsp white miso
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

### Title
- title: Soup Sundubu Jjigae

### Hardware
- Instant Pot/pressure cooker
### Instructions
- make [[_3.1.3. Quick Japanese Broth|quick Japanese broth]]
- add oil, onion, scallion whites, garlic, ginger, gochujang, soy sauce, sliced fresh mushrooms, and zucchini.
	- -> Stir well.
- cook on high pressure for 2 minutes + quick release.
- mix miso in a bit of broth, then add it to the mass
- add tofu and enoki mushrooms
- heat on low fire for 4 minutes
- add sesame oil
- finish with scallion greens
