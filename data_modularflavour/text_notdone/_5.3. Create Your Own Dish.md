### Text
I like DND, I like discovering new things, I like cooking. 
As such, when combining the three, I have devised a little game to construct a new dish each time.

Combine one item from each chapter into a new dish. Roll dice (or just choose numbers). And if you don't like it or don't have it, roll again. We're human after all.
Some components have been taken out because they're quite difficult to combine on their own.


#### How to play
Roll dies and figure out what you'll be making in the sections below. For slow discovery, roll them one by one, for explosiveness, roll all at one (2d6+1d4+1d12+1d10).
(If you're vegan, you'll have to reroll at times)
