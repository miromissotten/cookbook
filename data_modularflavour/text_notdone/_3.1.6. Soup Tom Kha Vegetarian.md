### Ingredients
- 500 g coconut milk
- 500 g vegetable stock
- 250 g mushrooms, sliced
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 20 g fresh ginger, sliced thinly
- zest of 1 lime
- zest of half a lemon
- 1 small strip orange peel
- 1 small chili, finely sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 45 g fresh lime juice
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 15 g cilantro
### Instructions
- fry the mushrooms until golden (about 8 minutes)
- add ginger, lime zest, lemon zest, orange peel, and chili. Stir for 30 seconds
- add the vegetable stock + simmer 10 minutes
- add coconut milk and tofu + simmer on low fire 5 minutes
- add soy sauce and sugar + remove from heat
- add the lime juice
- taste:
    - too heavy → add lime
    - too sour → add sugar
    - too boring → add soy sauce
 - finish with spring onion and cilantro
